### Priority qualifications for the current exhibit

1. **HOLD the 0.35–0.45 mm fang-length card.** The supplied Bücherl scan yielded no readable text and screenshots could not be retrieved; the original numerical passage, species, sex, sample and length convention were not verified. No replacement widow species-by-sex-by-instar series was recovered in this pass. The attribution is unverified, rather than demonstrated false. [bucherl1964]
2. **Retain zero only with its anatomical object.** Latrodectus lacks cheliceral groove-margin teeth; this does not mean no fangs, no setae, no endite serrula, or no microscopic surface relief anywhere. Direct brown-widow feeding observations and comparative morphology support toothlessness; Peralta is principally a bite case report. [eberhard2006; agnarsson2004; peralta2018]
3. **Split the female-bites sentence.** “Stronger biting muscles” is not established by the cited extension account or by body size alone; HOLD a measured widow male–female muscle-force comparison. Relatively larger glands in the female western-widow reconstruction are reported by the existing 2026 atlas citation, but its full treatment was not accessible here, so do not extract new dimensions or generalise its specimens into a genus-wide reserve ratio. [vetter2017; miller2026]
4. **Keep the male-bite counterexample, with case limits.** Peralta describes an identified adult male L. geometricus associated with a bite on thin upper-back skin and local effects; it overturns an absolute impossibility claim, not the distinction between that case and severe latrodectism. [peralta2018]
5. **Keep opposing fang muscles, but qualify whole-base mechanics.** A 2025 pressure-manipulation study includes three female L. hesperus and supports hydraulics at the basal articulation; “no hydraulics in biting” is now untenable as an unqualified spider claim. Its positional changes do not supply a living-widow pressure or force calibration. [wood2025]
6. **Do not add a generic lateral boss to the widow.** The comparative theridiid treatment codes the cheliceral boss absent in theridioids; that external feature is distinct from fang-hinge condyles. [agnarsson2004]
7. **Keep venom separate from digestive fluid.** The direct brown-widow observations contradict a sealed, pressure-driven digestive-fluid injection-and-suction model at the prey hole. [eberhard2006]
8. **Keep the orientation summary, remove a simple evolutionary ladder.** Labidognath and orthognath are useful comparative arrangements, not measured universal force/gape classes or a “primitive to perfect” progression. [foelix2011]
9. **Keep the subterminal outlet.** The canal opens short of the solid extreme tip; do not draw an open hypodermic bevel at the point or animate food returning through the venom canal. [snodgrass1952; foelix2011]

## 0. body.chelicerae — Chelicerae & Fangs

**Suggested kicker:** A hinged tool at the front of the body.

**Suggested summary:** Each chelicera carries a fang that folds against its base. Twelve halls follow this first appendage pair through its anatomy, cuticle, movement, growth and work, distinguishing direct widow observations from the spiders that supply our closest mechanical comparisons.

**Taxa studied:** LATRODECTUS: principally L. geometricus, L. hesperus and L. hasselti, with historical “L. mactans” anatomy explicitly identified. OTHER THERIDIID: Parasteatoda tepidariorum and taxonomic comparisons including Enoplognatha and hadrotarsines. OTHER SPIDER: principally Cupiennius salei for fang materials and developmental gene-expression comparisons; Araneus diadematus for comparative heavy-element materials. GENERAL SPIDER: conventional anatomy and orientation.

### OBSERVATIONS

- A widow's first prosomal appendage pair carries the venom-delivering fangs. **established — LATRODECTUS, anatomical placement** [foelix2011; snodgrass1952]
- Brown widows repeatedly regrasp feeding prey with their fangs. **supported — LATRODECTUS, L. geometricus, microscope observations** [eberhard2006]
- Western widows can bite without releasing detectable venom into an experimental target. **supported — LATRODECTUS, L. hesperus, defensive-bite assay** [nelsen2014]

These cards and observations orient the parent; the detailed anatomical distinctions belong in the halls.

### TERMS

- **Chelicera:** one of the paired first prosomal appendages; plural chelicerae.
- **Paturon:** basal segment supporting the articulated fang.
- **Fang:** distal puncturing segment, carrying the terminal part of the venom route.
- **Labidognath:** having the opposed cheliceral arrangement characteristic of araneomorphs.
- **Subterminal:** short of the extreme tip.

### Claims to avoid

- No calibrated widow fang length, pore offset, fang-hinge excursion, bite force or material gradient is proposed; experimentally induced basal-joint changes are available separately.
- “Zero teeth” cannot be shortened to “no teeth or cutting surfaces anywhere.”
- A duct within a fang does not make the complete fang a uniformly hollow, thin-walled syringe.
- A combined brown-widow exterior, historical venom route and Cupiennius material section must be labelled comparative.

## 1. body.chelicerae.first-appendage — The First Pair

**Suggested kicker:** First in the limb series, moving around the mouth.

**Suggested summary:** Chelicerae name the chelicerates, but their position changes as a spider develops. Widow embryos and comparative gene expression explain why the first biting appendage belongs beside the antennae in a segmental comparison, even though its adult job is very different.

**Taxa studied:** LATRODECTUS: L. geometricus embryonic surface anatomy. OTHER SPIDER: C. salei Hox expression. GENERAL SPIDER: adult appendage order and mouthpart nomenclature.

### OBSERVATIONS

- Chelicerae precede pedipalps in the spider's prosomal appendage series. **established — GENERAL SPIDER** [foelix2011]
- Brown-widow chelicerae remain posterior to the mouth through the study's Step 6. **supported — LATRODECTUS, L. geometricus, SEM series** [liu2009]
- They occupy a paraoral position at Step 7 in that series. **supported — LATRODECTUS, L. geometricus** [liu2009]
- In adult spiders the chelicerae lie in front of the mouth beneath the anterior carapace. **established — GENERAL SPIDER** [snodgrass1952]
- Hox-expression boundaries in Cupiennius support correspondence between the cheliceral and first-antennal segments. **supported — OTHER SPIDER, C. salei, comparative developmental inference** [damen1998]

### TERMS

- **Chelicerata:** arthropod group named for its chelicerae.
- **Deutocerebrum:** second brain neuromere in the comparative arthropod scheme.
- **Homology:** correspondence through shared ancestry, independent of present function.
- **Paraoral / preoral / postoral:** beside / ahead of / behind the mouth in the specified stage and view.
- **Clypeus:** carapace region between the anterior eyes and anterior edge.
- **Rostrum / labrum:** upper-lip terminology; identify the source's convention rather than adding two separate lips.

### Claims to avoid

- Do not label the chelicerae unconditionally “the first postoral adult limbs” or describe the deutocerebrum as missing.
- Hox similarity supports segmental correspondence; it does not show a widow embryo growing an insect antenna or prove a particular fossil's appendage identity.
- The first antenna, rather than the mandible or crustacean second antenna, is the relevant segmental comparator.
- No widow-specific Hox manipulation, gene-defined developmental clock or embryonic fate map of every adult paturon structure was verified.
- Signpost body.pedipalps.appendage for the second pair and body.digestion.mouth-and-pump for the lips, endites and actual oral opening.

## 2. body.chelicerae.paturon — The Basal Segment

**Suggested kicker:** A receiving groove and a working base.

**Suggested summary:** The stout paturon shelters the folded fang and houses its muscle machinery. Its groove, margins, setae and flexible attachment must be distinguished from the teeth and bosses seen in other spiders.

**Taxa studied:** LATRODECTUS: L. geometricus cheliceral preparation and genus diagnosis. OTHER THERIDIID: comparative tooth, hair and boss characters. GENERAL SPIDER: basal attachment and fang groove.

### OBSERVATIONS

- The folded fang rests in a groove on the basal segment. **established — GENERAL SPIDER** [foelix2011]
- The paturon connects to the anterior body wall through flexible integument. **established — GENERAL SPIDER, comparative anatomy** [snodgrass1952]
- Latrodectus chelicerae lack the groove teeth used in many spider diagnoses. **established — LATRODECTUS, generic diagnosis** [peralta2018]
- Most theridiids in the morphological survey retain promarginal teeth. **supported — OTHER THERIDIID, comparative character 106** [agnarsson2004]
- Distal mesial cheliceral hairs vary in serration among theridiid lineages. **supported — OTHER THERIDIID, comparative character 121** [agnarsson2004]
- The survey distinguishes a cheliceral boss from the fang and its articulation. **established — GENERAL SPIDER, morphological character definition** [agnarsson2004]

### TERMS

- **Fang furrow / groove:** receiving recess for the folded fang.
- **Promargin:** anterior margin of that groove in the conventional anatomical account.
- **Retromargin:** posterior margin, to be recorded separately.
- **Seta:** cuticular hair, distinct from a fixed tooth.
- **Boss:** particular external basal prominence used in comparative morphology.
- **Condyle:** articular contact forming part of a joint; not a synonym for the external boss.

### Claims to avoid

- HOLD absolute widow paturon dimensions, male:female proportional width and a genus-wide setal count; overall body-size dimorphism supplies none of these measurements.
- A smooth margin is not evidence that the surface is hairless or untextured at SEM scale.
- Do not put a generic spider boss on Latrodectus or turn its absence into absence of a functional fang hinge.
- HOLD a fixed carapace–paturon hinge pin: the flexible basal attachment permits more complex motion than the distal fang hinge.
- Signpost body.legs.segments-and-joints for joint vocabulary and Hall 6 for muscle/pressure mechanics.

## 3. body.chelicerae.fang — The Fang Itself

**Suggested kicker:** The sharp point extends beyond the outlet.

**Suggested summary:** A fang is a curved cuticular puncturing tool traversed by a venom route. Its canal, solid point, receiving groove and hinge are separate features; the best measured cross-sections come from a much larger non-widow spider.

**Taxa studied:** LATRODECTUS: L. geometricus exterior preparation and historical “L. mactans” duct drawing. OTHER SPIDER: C. salei micro-CT/section studies. GENERAL SPIDER: fang articulation and outlet terminology.

### FIGURES

|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | distal fang per chelicera | One movable puncturing segment | General spider plan | [foelix2011] |
| Subterminal | outlet position | Solid point beyond pore | No measured Latrodectus pore-to-tip distance proposed | [snodgrass1952] |
| 2 | mm, fitted centreline radius | A measured comparator | Adult C. salei micro-CT model, R; not widow fang length | [baron2014] |

### OBSERVATIONS

- The spider fang is the articulated distal segment of the chelicera. **established — GENERAL SPIDER** [foelix2011]
- The venom duct traverses the fang to an opening near its tip. **established — GENERAL SPIDER, comparative anatomy including historical widow figure** [snodgrass1952]
- The extreme point lies beyond the canal outlet. **established — GENERAL SPIDER** [foelix2011]
- Cupiennius micro-CT reveals a curved canal enclosed within the fang wall. **supported — OTHER SPIDER, C. salei** [baron2014]
- That reconstructed fang has an imperfectly axisymmetric cross-section. **supported — OTHER SPIDER, C. salei** [baron2014]
- Its wall and canal dimensions vary along the fang. **supported — OTHER SPIDER, C. salei, geometry reconstruction** [baron2014]

### TERMS

- **Venom canal:** distal passage inside the fang.
- **Lumen:** internal space of a duct, distinct from its wall.
- **Subterminal pore:** outlet proximal to the solid extreme point.
- **Cross-section:** slice normal to a specified axis; changes with position along a curved fang.
- **Centreline radius:** geometric curve parameter, distinct from arc length or tip-to-base chord.
- **Serrula:** usually the denticulate edge of a palpal endite in spider mouthpart descriptions.

### Claims to avoid

- **HOLD 0.35–0.45 mm [bucherl1964]** and all uncited widow species/sex/instar fang-length ranges; neither a printed scale bar nor a fitted curve radius is a whole-fang measurement.
- Foelix's serrated fang-edge illustration is Cupiennius; HOLD its presence, tooth spacing and silk-cutting function in Latrodectus until a widow preparation documents them.
- Do not transfer a palpal endite's serrula onto the fang or use groove toothlessness to exclude every possible fang-edge microstructure.
- “Hollow” describes an internal canal, not an empty fang with a hole through the extreme point.
- Signpost Hall 4 for cuticle and Hall 7 for the continuous duct; the body.digestion mouth is a separate opening.

## 4. body.chelicerae.fang-materials — Metal-Tipped

### TERMS

- **Cuticle:** secreted external composite, not a metal exoskeleton.
- **Sclerotisation:** chemical hardening of the organic matrix.
- **Histidine:** amino acid whose side group can coordinate metal ions.
- **Coordination bond:** metal–ligand association, distinct from a metallic coating.
- **Hardness:** resistance to local permanent indentation.
- **Reduced indentation modulus:** test-derived elastic response, distinct from force or fracture toughness.

### Claims to avoid

- “Widow fangs are zinc-tipped” remains HOLD as a direct chemical claim; this is a comparative material explanation.
- Do not turn manganese in spider claws or halogens in other invertebrate tools into measured widow-fang constituents.
- Lichtenegger's Nereis work concerns marine worms; its exact zinc coordination must not be assigned automatically to spiders. [lichtenegger2003; politi2012]
- Hardness alone does not establish abrasion, crack resistance or functional lifespan; HOLD widow wear rates, sharpening and self-repair.
- Neither a metal cap nor uniform mineralisation describes the proposed zinc–protein association; hydration and test direction matter.
- Signpost life.molting and Hall 10 for replacement, body.legs.feet-and-grip for foot claws.

### IMAGE BRIEFS

- **1536×1024 museum plate:** near-black background, cream linework; explicitly labelled C. salei comparison, base-to-tip sections with patterned Zn/Cl/Ca distributions and separate material-property cards; no metallic cap or widow chemistry label.
- **1536×1024 museum plate:** postmolt matrix before and after zinc incorporation, proposed transport channels distinct from the large venom canal; indicate interpretation rather than a filmed ion flow.

## 5. body.chelicerae.labidognath — Pincers, Not Picks

**Suggested kicker:** Two tools approach from opposite sides.

**Suggested summary:** Widow fangs oppose one another in the araneomorph arrangement. Comparing this with a mygalomorph separates principal closure directions from the more complicated movements of the bases and keeps orientation distinct from strength.

**Taxa studied:** LATRODECTUS: L. hesperus bite descriptions and experimental basal movement. OTHER SPIDER: mygalomorph/araneomorph comparators. GENERAL SPIDER: labidognath, orthognath and plagiognath terminology.

### FIGURES

| 2 | opposed fangs | Sideways approach | Widow/araneomorph arrangement | [foelix2011; nelsen2014] |
| Opposed / roughly parallel | orientations | Araneomorph / mygalomorph | Conventional categories; oblique arrangements complicate the binary | [foelix2011] |
| +7.2 | degrees, mean change in gape | Bases spread under raised pressure | L. hesperus, three freshly killed females; delta, not maximum fang opening | [wood2025] |

### OBSERVATIONS

- The widow's labidognath arrangement brings the fangs toward one another. **established — LATRODECTUS, observed defensive bite** [nelsen2014]
- Typical mygalomorph fangs act in roughly parallel longitudinal planes. **established — GENERAL SPIDER, comparative orientation** [foelix2011]
- Some spider arrangements are oblique rather than strictly parallel or opposed. **established — GENERAL SPIDER, plagiognath comparison** [foelix2011]
- Whole cheliceral bases can move beyond the principal fang-closing plane. **established — GENERAL SPIDER, functional anatomy** [foelix2011]
- Raised pressure increased intercheliceral gape in the sampled western widows. **supported — LATRODECTUS, L. hesperus, three females in postmortem manipulation** [wood2025]
- Greater prey span is not established as a universal advantage of labidognathy in the comparative account. **supported — GENERAL SPIDER, evaluation of an evolutionary hypothesis** [foelix2011]

### TERMS

- **Labidognath:** opposed arrangement of the cheliceral apparatus.
- **Orthognath:** roughly parallel arrangement associated with mygalomorphs.
- **Plagiognath:** oblique arrangement in the cited anatomical usage.
- **Gape:** separation angle or distance between explicitly defined landmarks.
- **Adduction / abduction:** movement toward / away from the midline.
- **Levation / depression:** raising / lowering relative to body axes.

### Claims to avoid

- The title concerns principal working directions; tarantula bases can also spread sideways.
- Do not animate an araneomorph as a tarantula with its jaws rotated through a known evolutionary angle; no transformation angle/date is proposed.
- HOLD widow force advantages, reach multipliers, prey-size thresholds and live maximum gape; orientation supplies none of these measurements.
- The pressure-induced +7.2° belongs to the bases, not the fang–paturon hinge, and is not a bite filmed in vivo.
- Signpost genus.family-tree for phylogeny, Hall 6 for motors and web.wrapping for silk restraint.

### IMAGE BRIEFS

- **1536×1024 museum plate:** orientation comparison with an oblique inset, without a ladder of increasing perfection, invented rotation scale or orb behind the widow.
