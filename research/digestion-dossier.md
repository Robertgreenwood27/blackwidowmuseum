## Reading this dossier

- Bracketed citation keys refer to content/sources.yaml or the NEW entries in SOURCES below. research/silk-glands-dossier.md and research/hourglass-dossier.md were read first, followed by the digestion case, source registry and the fifteen requested neighbouring cases. Registry presence alone was not treated as verification.
- **established** = directly documented and secure as qualified; **supported** = a particular experiment, limited sample or interpretation; **debated** = a mechanism or explanation requiring further tests. Confidence applies to the qualified sentence, not to every widow.
- Evidence classes are **LATRODECTUS**, **OTHER THERIDIID**, **OTHER SPIDER**, and **GENERAL SPIDER**. A textbook account of the spider gut is GENERAL SPIDER, even when used to explain a widow; a named comparator's dissection is OTHER SPIDER. Mixed studies identify both classes where applicable.
- FIGURES are candidate fact cards, not illustrations. Means, errors, sample identity, prey, assay and conditions travel with the number. Qualitative cards are used when no defensible measurement is available; blank knowledge is not a zero.
- Distinguish capture, feeding on a prey package, absorption, intracellular digestion, nutrient storage and waste elimination. A discarded prey shell never passed through the spider. Likewise, prey mass offered is not mass swallowed, pulse frequency is not intake rate, and metabolic expenditure is not food intake.
- Names follow the checked [World Spider Catalog genus listing](https://wsc.nmbe.ch/species-list/3681/Latrodectus). Historical specimens retain the source's identification; “L. mactans” in old work is not automatically a modern, voucher-verified southern widow. The redback is styled *L. hasselti*, even where a paper prints *hasseltii*. [wsc2026]
- Primary experiments, microscopy and field collections anchor the dossier; general anatomy is explicitly separated from widow measurements. Where only a primary abstract or indexed publication text was retrievable, its access limits are recorded below. These limits do not justify inventing a missing sample size or condition.
- Every widow scene must use a three-dimensional tangle cobweb, with substrate-attached gumfoot lines where relevant, never an orb. A hanging female's hourglass is on the abdominal venter and faces upward because she is inverted; it is not a dorsal marking. Internal false colours are diagrammatic.
- This case owns food processing and its physiological consequences. Capture choreography belongs in **web.wrapping** and **body.comb-foot**; venom delivery and chemistry in **body.chelicerae**, **venom.prey** and **venom.venom-gland**; web allocation in **web.hunger**; silk production in **body.silk-glands.life-and-supply**; reproduction and development in **life.***.

### Priority qualifications for the current exhibit

1. Revise “floods her prey” using Hall 1's qualified observations of fluid exchange.
2. Replace the causal promise “and can then go many months” with species-, sex-, stage- and temperature-qualified survival experiments; a single meal was not shown to purchase a fixed fasting interval.
3. Preserve Hall 1's pulse-count sample; movement frequency is not volumetric intake.
4. Relabel the mouth filter, pump, diverticula and Malpighian statements as general spider anatomy unless a specific widow preparation supports the detail.
5. Describe digestion as extra-oral **and** internal; “all digestion occurs outside” is false. [fuzita2016]
6. Distinguish digestive fluid returning through the mouth from venom leaving the fang duct; molecular overlap does not make the two secretions identical. [walter2017]
7. Preserve >300 and 160 days as reported redback experimental maxima, with incomplete treatment metadata flagged; prefer the fully identified Kaston cohort for a launch card. [forster1989; kaston1970]
8. Do not call all abdominal enlargement stored fat, all white material guanine, every bacterium a gut mutualist, or discarded prey a faecal pellet.

## 0. body.digestion — Liquid Meals

**Suggested kicker:** A meal continues after the prey is gone.

**Suggested summary:** Widows process food outside the mouth, filter and pump it inward, and use a branching digestive system to absorb and retain nutrients. Twelve halls follow the meal from a prey package to reserves, fasting and wastes, keeping direct widow evidence separate from comparative spider anatomy.

**Taxa studied:** LATRODECTUS: *L. geometricus*, *L. hesperus*, *L. hasselti* and source-identified *L. mactans* for the overview; additional species and named comparators appear in individual halls.

### FIGURES

| Value | Unit | Short label | Qualifier | Sources |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Outside + inside | digestive phases | More than predigestion | GENERAL SPIDER framework, with molecular confirmation in *Nephilingis cruentata*, not a widow cell atlas | [fuzita2016] |
| 36–193 | days unfed | A measured fasting cohort | *L. hesperus*, 37 females deprived from adult molt; laboratory room temperature, not constant 25 °C | [kaston1970] |

### OBSERVATIONS

- Brown widows repeatedly regurgitated fluid and took it back up while feeding. **supported — LATRODECTUS, L. geometricus, microscopy** [eberhard2006]
- Mated redback females reduced resting CO₂ output during food deprivation. **supported — LATRODECTUS, L. hasselti, respirometry** [stoltz2010b]
- Brown-widow droppings carried prey-derived isotope label in a nutrient-transfer experiment. **supported — LATRODECTUS, L. geometricus** [nishi2013]

These two cards and three observations orient the parent; the deep evidence belongs in the halls.

### TERMS

- **Extra-oral digestion:** breakdown outside the mouth.
- **Assimilation:** incorporation of absorbed material into the animal's physiological economy; operational definitions differ among experiments.
- **Reserve:** stored material available for later use, not simply a recently filled gut.
- **Excretion:** disposal of metabolic waste, distinct from leaving uneaten prey outside.

### Claims to avoid

- “Cannot chew” describes the widow's feeding mode, not an absence of fang movement or prey puncturing, and must not become a claim that all spiders lack mastication.
- Avoid one diagram that presents every comparative observation as a dissection of *L. hesperus*.
- Do not make fasting endurance a recommended feeding schedule.

### IMAGE BRIEFS

- Make a source-checked widow cutaway connecting prey surface, mouth, prosomal pump, branching midgut and posterior waste outlet; give comparative microscopic insets their own taxon labels.
- Show a hanging female in a tangle web with a wrapped fly, a separate spent prey shell and a droplet below the web; distinguish three objects instead of turning all residues into “poop.”

## 1. body.digestion.extra-oral — A Meal Outside the Mouth

**Suggested kicker:** Wet, withdraw, repeat.

**Suggested summary:** A close view follows fluid release and uptake, separating observed movements from proposed transport mechanisms. The illustration should distinguish the prey surface, prey interior and mouth.

**Taxa studied:** LATRODECTUS: two adult female *L. geometricus*, collected near San José, Costa Rica, observed at room temperature with three approximately 50–100 mg muscoid flies; filming at 30 frames/s. OTHER SPIDER: *Nephilingis cruentata* for the molecular origin of digestive fluid.

### FIGURES

| Value | Unit | Short label | Qualifier | Sources |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 4.9–7.4 | uptake pulses/s | A visible feeding rhythm | One brown widow; means of three bouts, 76 pulses total | [eberhard2006] |

### OBSERVATIONS

- Rapid regurgitation alternated with slower pulsing uptake. **supported — LATRODECTUS, L. geometricus** [eberhard2006]
- Liquid accumulated on the cheliceral surfaces without a mouth-to-prey seal. **supported — LATRODECTUS, L. geometricus** [eberhard2006]
- Fly bodies remained largely intact except at feeding holes. **supported — LATRODECTUS, L. geometricus** [eberhard2006]
- Regrasping with the fangs accompanied fluid release. **supported — LATRODECTUS, L. geometricus** [eberhard2006]
- Delayed upward movement of fluid within a fly supported capillary entry. **supported — LATRODECTUS, L. geometricus, inferred mechanism** [eberhard2006]
- Matched gut transcriptomics and fluid proteomics supported midgut diverticula as the digestive-fluid source. **supported — OTHER SPIDER, N. cruentata** [fuzita2016]

### TERMS

- **Regurgitation:** return of alimentary fluid through the mouth.
- **Capillarity:** movement governed by surface tension and wetting in narrow spaces.
- **Prey package:** prey and surrounding silk, distinct from the spider's own gut.
- **Mastication:** mechanical crushing or grinding of food.

### Claims to avoid

- Capillarity is a supported explanation, not a direct pressure measurement or a complete fluid-mechanics model.
- Do not show digestive fluid emerging from the fang's venom pore.
- Maxillary-gland contributions are proposed in comparative accounts, not quantified for these widows; no percentage contribution or widow secretion map was verified. [snodgrass1952]
- Whole-package saturation and long mouthpart-free feeding in *Philoponella* must not be redrawn as observations of *Latrodectus*.
- No net intake volume, enzyme dose, fraction recovered or complete extraction efficiency was measured in this brown-widow sequence.
- Signpost **web.wrapping** for preparing the package and **body.chelicerae** for fang articulation.

### IMAGE BRIEFS

- Make a two-phase brown-widow feeding close-up showing an external liquid bridge and separately labelled release/uptake arrows, with no sealed straw or digestive outlet through a fang.
- Pair a largely intact spent fly with feeding holes and a conceptual cross-section of fluid moving within it; label capillary transport as an interpretation, and leave the chitinous shell visible.

## 2. body.digestion.mouth-and-pump — The Gate and the Pump

**Suggested kicker:** Filter first, pull inward.

**Suggested summary:** Mouthpart hairs and the pharyngeal filter screen food before a muscular foregut pump draws liquid inward. The comparative anatomy is detailed, but a measured widow mouth aperture, filter cutoff and complete pump-muscle map remain separate verification tasks.

**Taxa studied:** GENERAL SPIDER: mouthpart terminology and cuticle-lined foregut; OTHER SPIDER: *Grammostola cala* as identified by Dunlop, and *Agelena naevia*, *Ancylometes* and *Argiope trifasciata* in Snodgrass's comparative plates. LATRODECTUS: *L. geometricus* embryonic mouth-region SEM, not adult aperture measurements.

### FIGURES

| Value | Unit | Short label | Qualifier | Sources |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| About 1 | µm particle scale | Fine screening | GENERAL SPIDER literature estimate, not a measured widow mouth width or sharp universal cutoff | [eberhard2006] |
| Setae → palate plate | successive filters | Two screening levels | Comparative spider anatomy; individual bristle spacing not quantified here | [dunlop1994] |
| Not verified | µm aperture | Widow mouth size | Search outcome; no adult widow measurement adopted | — |

### OBSERVATIONS

- Spider endites are modified pedipalp coxae bordering the mouth laterally. **established — GENERAL SPIDER** [foelix2011]
- The rostrum lies anterior to the mouth and the labium posterior to it. **established — GENERAL SPIDER, anatomical terminology** [foelix2011]
- Dense inner endite setae filter incoming food. **established — GENERAL SPIDER** [foelix2011]
- Serrated pharyngeal platelets flank a median groove in the examined tarantula. **established — OTHER SPIDER, G. cala, SEM** [dunlop1994]
- Brown-widow embryogenesis produces a slit-like mouth overhung by the hypostome. **established — LATRODECTUS, L. geometricus, developmental SEM** [liu2009]
- The oesophagus passes through the central nerve mass before reaching the posterior prosomal pump. **established — GENERAL SPIDER, comparative anatomy** [snodgrass1952]
- Dorsal pump dilators attach to a carapace apodeme and lateral dilators to the endosternum. **established — OTHER SPIDER, A. naevia muscle illustration** [snodgrass1952]
- Short compressor muscles act on the pump's sides. **established — OTHER SPIDER, A. naevia** [snodgrass1952]
- The foregut is lined by cuticle. **established — GENERAL SPIDER** [foelix2011]

### TERMS

- **Labium:** the lower lip behind the mouth, not the homolog of an insect's fused maxilla-like labium.
- **Endite / gnathocoxa / maxilla:** terms used for the feeding expansion of a pedipalp base; specify the structure rather than implying an independent chewing jaw.
- **Rostrum / upper lip:** the anterior mouth-region structure; hypostome and labrum usage differs among anatomical traditions and embryological papers.
- **Pharynx:** the flattened anterior foregut behind the mouth.
- **Sucking stomach / proventricular pump:** the muscular foregut expansion, distinct from the absorptive midgut.
- **Apodeme:** an internal cuticular projection providing a muscle attachment.
- **Endosternite / endosternum:** the internal supporting plate in the prosoma, not the external sternum.

### Claims to avoid

- The micron-scale filter estimate is neither fang-pore diameter nor mouth aperture; “no particle whatsoever can enter” is stronger than the evidence.
- Do not label every mouthpart hair a filter: position and morphology matter.
- Filtration does not sterilize a meal, and fluid feeding does not imply an empty microbiome.
- Do not import the tarantula's tooth row, palate dimensions or exact muscle counts into a widow.
- Pump expansion draws fluid into the foregut; it does not prove vacuum suction from a sealed prey interior.
- No widow pump stroke volume, negative pressure, duty cycle, adult mouth width or measured filter-retention curve was verified.
- Keep fang opening/closing in **body.chelicerae**, foregut cuticle shedding in the context of **life.molting**, and hemolymph hydraulics in **body.circulation**.

### IMAGE BRIEFS

- Draw an oriented ventral mouthpart map with chelicerae partly removed to reveal endites, labium and anterior rostrum; put a taxon-labelled tarantula palate SEM schematic beside it, not inside an allegedly measured widow.
- Make a comparative sagittal pump plate locating pharynx, oesophagus, nerve mass, sucking stomach, carapace attachment and endosternite; distinguish food-lumen arrows from hemolymph flow and mark the muscle arrangement's comparator source.

## 3. body.digestion.midgut — The Branching Larder

**Suggested kicker:** Absorption and storage share living tissue.

**Suggested summary:** The midgut is a branching organ system, not a straight pipe ending in a simple stomach bag. Comparative microscopy and tracer experiments show how its cells finish digestion and handle reserves; widow histology and lipoprotein chemistry provide narrower direct anchors.

**Taxa studied:** LATRODECTUS: genus-identified black-widow histological case in Gaudette et al.; *L. mirabilis* hemolymph. GENERAL SPIDER: branching gut and cell framework. OTHER SPIDER: *Polybetes pythagoricus* (paper abstract also spells phythagoricus) for lipid tracing; *Nephilingis cruentata* for digestive-fluid origin.

### FIGURES

| Value | Unit | Short label | Qualifier | Sources |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Secretory + resorptive | principal epithelial roles | Cells doing different jobs | GENERAL SPIDER framework; not a complete two-cell-only widow atlas | [laino2009] |
| 80 | % of plasma lipids | Lipids travel in a carrier | *L. mirabilis*, isolated HDL₁ fraction; not abdominal fat percentage | [cunningham2000] |
| Prosoma + abdomen | gut distribution | Beyond one body compartment | GENERAL SPIDER anatomy; leg-base branches are not a widow whole-leg atlas | [snodgrass1952] |

### OBSERVATIONS

- Anterior midgut diverticula branch toward the leg bases. **established — GENERAL SPIDER, comparative anatomy** [snodgrass1952]
- Branched abdominal diverticula occupy much of the upper abdomen. **established — GENERAL SPIDER** [snodgrass1952]
- Normal midgut-diverticulum histology is illustrated from a black widow identified only to genus. **established — LATRODECTUS, Latrodectus sp., case 5, Fig. 3a inset** [gaudette2023]
- Secretory cells release digestive material into the gut lumen. **established — GENERAL SPIDER, histological framework** [fuzita2016]
- Resorptive cells internalize predigested material into vacuoles for intracellular processing. **established — GENERAL SPIDER, histological framework** [laino2009]
- Lipid and glycogen storage in the digestive system had histological support before tracer studies. **supported — GENERAL SPIDER, historical evidence explicitly distinguished by the authors** [laino2009]
- Labelled palmitate accumulated mainly in midgut diverticula after feeding or injection. **supported — OTHER SPIDER, P. pythagoricus, radioisotope experiment** [laino2009]
- Those diverticula synthesized several lipid classes from the label. **supported — OTHER SPIDER, P. pythagoricus** [laino2009]
- Two high-density lipoprotein fractions were isolated from *L. mirabilis* hemolymph. **established — LATRODECTUS, L. mirabilis, biochemical fractionation** [cunningham2000]

### TERMS

- **Diverticulum / caecum:** a blind-ended outgrowth connected to the gut lumen; plural diverticula / caeca.
- **Midgut gland / digestive mass:** the branched digestive tissue; “hepatopancreas” is a functional analogy, not a separate vertebrate liver and pancreas.
- **Pinocytosis:** cellular uptake of extracellular fluid in membrane-bound vesicles.
- **Digestive vacuole:** an intracellular compartment in which ingested material is processed.
- **Triacylglycerol:** a major storage-lipid form.
- **Glycogen:** a glucose-based storage carbohydrate.
- **Lipoprotein:** a lipid–protein particle transporting lipid in an aqueous fluid.

### Claims to avoid

- The larder includes tissue reserves; retained gut liquid is not automatically stored lipid or glycogen.
- Do not make the entire abdomen digestive tissue: ovaries, silk glands, heart, lungs and other organs also require space.
- “Toward/into the leg bases” does not establish caeca in every widow leg segment or a measured adult widow distribution.
- Do not call the two principal epithelial roles the only cells: interstitial and other specialized cells complicate the anatomy.
- No widow-specific cell-resolved uptake atlas, lipid/glycogen concentration series or organ-volume budget was verified.
- The *Polybetes* tracer result does not establish the same partition percentages, mobilization timetable or carrier proportions in widows.
- Signpost **body.circulation** for the transport system; this hall owns nutrient handling, not a second heart exhibit.

### IMAGE BRIEFS

- Make a transparent gut map with prosomal and abdominal branches, leaving other organ spaces visible; use dotted, explicitly comparative leg-base extensions instead of filling each widow leg with orange gut.
- Pair a genus-identified widow histology reference with separately labelled *Polybetes* tracer arrows and a secretory/resorptive cell schematic; label lipid droplets and glycogen as distinct reserve forms without invented quantities.

## 4. body.digestion.enzymes — A Mixed Chemical Kitchen

**Suggested kicker:** Breaking tissue takes more than one enzyme.

**Suggested summary:** Molecular work in other spiders reveals a varied digestive secretion and a second enzymatic phase within cells. Widow venom datasets supply relevant molecular leads, but they are not a digestive-fluid proteome or proof that venom alone liquefies a meal.

**Taxa studied:** OTHER SPIDER: *Nephilingis cruentata*, *Stegodyphus mimosarum* and *Acanthoscurria geniculata*. LATRODECTUS: *L. hesperus* venom-gland, silk-gland and cephalothorax transcriptomics plus venom proteomics; no equivalent widow digestive-fluid dataset verified.

### FIGURES

| Value | Unit | Short label | Qualifier | Sources |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 25 | astacins detected in fluid | A protease-rich secretion | *N. cruentata*, proteomic identifications; not widow gene count | [fuzita2016] |
| 527 / 305 | identified proteins | Two comparative fluid proteomes | *S. mimosarum* / *A. geniculata*; all detections, not all active digestive enzymes | [walter2017] |
| Not verified | widow digestive proteomes | A remaining evidence gap | Venom and silk datasets do not substitute for gut secretion sampling | — |

### OBSERVATIONS

- The *Nephilingis* fluid contained peptidases, carbohydrases, lipases and a nuclease. **established — OTHER SPIDER, N. cruentata, proteomics** [fuzita2016]
- Chitinase was detected in the *Nephilingis* digestive fluid. **established — OTHER SPIDER, N. cruentata** [fuzita2016]
- Astacin-like metalloproteases were prominent among comparative fluid proteins. **supported — OTHER SPIDER, S. mimosarum and A. geniculata** [walter2017]
- The two comparative species differed in venom–digestive-fluid protein overlap. **supported — OTHER SPIDER, paired proteomic comparison** [walter2017]
- Western-widow venom-gland-enriched transcripts included chitinase and protease candidates. **supported — LATRODECTUS, L. hesperus, transcript annotation** [haney2014]
- That widow experiment compared venom glands with silk glands and cephalothorax. **established — LATRODECTUS, L. hesperus, tissue sampling** [haney2014]

### TERMS

- **Protease / peptidase:** enzyme cleaving peptide bonds in proteins or peptides.
- **Astacin:** a family of zinc-dependent metalloproteases.
- **Lipase:** enzyme hydrolysing lipid substrates.
- **Carbohydrase:** enzyme acting on carbohydrate substrates.
- **Chitinase:** enzyme cleaving chitin; detection does not establish complete digestion of hardened insect cuticle.
- **Nuclease:** enzyme breaking down nucleic acids.
- **Transcriptome / proteome / activity assay:** sampled RNA / detected proteins / measured catalysis; three different evidence levels.

### Claims to avoid

- Do not publish a “widow recipe” with these comparator counts, concentrations or substrate optima.
- An annotated protease transcript may have roles in toxin maturation, cellular maintenance or digestion; annotation alone does not settle its in vivo substrate.
- Chitinase does not mean a widow consumes every part of a beetle shell, and lack of shell ingestion does not mean chitinases are absent.
- A protein found in both secretions does not establish identical mixtures, concentrations, routes or functions.
- No verified widow digestive-fluid pH series, complete enzyme inventory, enzyme replenishment curve or paired gut-versus-venom activity comparison was found in this pass.
- **venom.prey** owns toxin targeting; **venom.venom-gland** owns venom production and delivery. Do not move their mechanistic content here.

### IMAGE BRIEFS

- Draw a substrate-to-enzyme plate for protein, lipid, carbohydrate and nucleic acid, explicitly headed “comparative spider digestive-fluid evidence”; use a separate widow “dataset still needed” label.
- Show mouth-derived digestive fluid and fang-derived venom as distinct routes, joined only by an overlapping-protein inset sourced to the named comparators; do not draw two unrelated mixtures as identical bottles.

## 5. body.digestion.menu — What Counts as Dinner?

**Suggested kicker:** A broad menu can have local favourites.

**Suggested summary:** Insects dominate several measured widow diets, but isopods, spiders and occasional vertebrates broaden the picture. Local prey collections establish diet composition; spectacular photographs establish particular events and cannot supply a normal feeding frequency.

**Taxa studied:** LATRODECTUS: *L. hesperus* in coastal British Columbia; *L. lilianae* in southeastern Spain; *L. mirabilis* in Argentine cereal fields; *L. hasselti* in Australian field observations and feeding trials; *L. pallidus* and *L. revivensis* in Israeli records; additional widow species in the mammal-record compilation.

### OBSERVATIONS

- Western-widow prey remains included insects, isopods and other spiders. **supported — LATRODECTUS, L. hesperus, one-year coastal field collection** [salomon2011]
- Beetles and hymenopterans dominated that western-widow sample. **supported — LATRODECTUS, L. hesperus** [salomon2011]

- Small-mammal reports included mice captured by several widow species. **supported — LATRODECTUS, L. hasselti, L. geometricus, L. hesperus and L. mactans, heterogeneous record compilation** [nyffeler2018]
- Published records document snake consumption by widows. **supported — LATRODECTUS, species-qualified records in a broader spider review** [nyffeler2021]
- Redbacks extracted nutrients less efficiently from fence skinks than from locusts in feeding trials. **supported — LATRODECTUS, L. hasselti, prey comparison** [wilder2022]

### TERMS

- **Polyphagy:** feeding on multiple prey types; not identical intake of every available animal.
- **Araneophagy:** eating spiders.
- **Intraguild predation:** eating another predator that shares some prey resources.
- **Frequency versus biomass:** counts of prey individuals versus their contribution by mass.
- **Prey remains / mummy:** a persistent residue used to reconstruct meals, with preservation and identification biases.

### Claims to avoid

- Do not turn an ant-heavy local sample into obligate ant specialization or assume the British Columbia diet describes all widows.
- A lizard, snake or mouse in a web is not automatically proof of completed feeding; distinguish capture, killing and consumption in each record.
- Scorpions in a genus-level Israeli list do not establish a scorpion fraction for each named widow species.
- “Vertebrate prey are always negligible” also overstates the evidence: the sampled urban redback population in Wilder and Simpson had repeated skink records, without establishing a genus-wide norm.
- Do not calculate prey-to-widow mass ratios from length, photographs, generic mouse weights or the 25 mm lizard record.
- No genus-wide maximum prey ratio or single “largest possible meal” was verified; mammal compilations mix observation types and reporting effort.
- Diet composition is not venom specificity, capture probability or nutrient assimilation. Signpost **venom.prey**, **web.wrapping** and **web.guests**; cannibalism mechanisms remain in **life.cannibalism** and **life.siblings**.

### IMAGE BRIEFS

- Build a locality-labelled menu plate with British Columbia, Spain and Argentine samples in separate trays; use real prey groups and distinguish count from biomass without fabricating a pooled diet chart.
- Place the documented *L. pallidus*–*Mesalina* event in a separate natural-history window with a scale bar and voucher citation; keep the everyday arthropod menu dominant and use a tangle web with the widow inverted.

## 6. body.digestion.meal-clock — A Meal in Hours

**Suggested kicker:** Feeding, digestion and waste run on different clocks.

**Suggested summary:** A meal is an extended process, not a single bite. Direct widow experiments connect prey handling with later metabolic work, while redback observations show feeding at multiple sites after transport to shelter.

**Taxa studied:** LATRODECTUS: laboratory-reared adult female *L. mactans*, mothers from Stillwater, Oklahoma, 25 ± 1 °C, 14L:10D, 14-day fast; *Tenebrio molitor* larvae (n = 14 spiders) versus adults (n = 15). These conditions also govern the Barnes energy card in Hall 8. Redback observations use *L. hasselti*; the lizard duration uses one *L. pallidus*.

### FIGURES

|---|---|---|---|---|

### OBSERVATIONS

- Handling time did not differ detectably between the two mealworm stages. **supported — LATRODECTUS, L. mactans, specified assay** [barnes2019]
- More lipid and protein were extracted from larvae than adult beetles. **supported — LATRODECTUS, L. mactans** [barnes2019]
- Redbacks transported wrapped prey to the retreat before feeding. **supported — LATRODECTUS, L. hasselti, behavioural account** [forster1995]
- Redbacks fed at sites around the head, body and leg joints. **supported — LATRODECTUS, L. hasselti** [forster1995]
- Gravid redbacks accumulated prey within retreats in the observational account. **supported — LATRODECTUS, L. hasselti** [forster1995]

### TERMS

- **Handling time:** an operational time associated with processing prey; retain each study's start, end and observation schedule.
- **Intake rate:** material ingested per unit time; requires volume or mass, not just movement counts.
- **Satiation:** a feeding state or experimental treatment; not a standard abdominal diameter.
- **Specific dynamic action (SDA):** metabolic expenditure associated with processing a meal above a baseline.
- **Cache:** prey retained outside the body, distinct from internal nutrient reserves.

### Claims to avoid

- Eight hours is not a universal widow digestion time or a high-resolution estimate of continuous sucking.
- The Barnes Methods mention generally finishing extra-oral feeding sooner, while Table 2 gives the handling means retained here; preserve the operational endpoint and do not conflate the two phrasings.
- No verified widow net µL/min intake rate or maximum meal mass swallowed was found; the prey offered ratio is not a meal-induced mass-gain ratio.
- A dry-looking discarded shell need not be chemically empty, and different prey stages differ in both nutrients and exoskeleton.
- Feeding can end before internal processing ends; a duration of elevated respiration is not a direct gut-emptying measurement.
- Do not turn prey caching into evidence of refrigeration, preservation chemistry or a measured safe storage duration.
- Satiation effects on web investment are owned by **web.hunger**; attack and hauling mechanics by **web.wrapping**.

### IMAGE BRIEFS

- Make a laboratory meal timeline with sparse observation ticks, prey handling and a separate post-meal physiology lane; show uncertainty between checks rather than a precision stopwatch.
- Show a redback at several sequential feeding sites on one prey item in her retreat; use anatomical landmarks instead of inventing a fixed bite count or identical site order.

## 7. body.digestion.fasting — Going Without

**Suggested kicker:** Endurance depends on the animal and the conditions.

**Suggested summary:** Female widows can survive long food interruptions in laboratory trials, but endurance differs with temperature, maturity and sex. Recovery experiments matter as much as maxima: survival can involve substantial loss of activity and reproductive output.

### TERMS

- **Malpighian tubules:** excretory tubes communicating with the gut; do not assume insect homology or identical developmental origin.
- **Stercoral pocket / sac / cloaca:** terms applied to the enlarged posterior collecting region; compare the original anatomical definitions before mapping a boundary.
- **Guanine:** a purine involved in spider nitrogen disposal and, in stored crystalline form, reflective coloration.

- **Coxal gland:** a paired excretory/osmoregulatory organ system with outlets associated with leg bases, separate from the alimentary outlet.
- **Nephrocyte:** a term for specialized sequestration/filtering cells in arthropods; a widow-specific anatomical and functional assignment was not verified here.
- **Prey remains:** uneaten external material; “egesta” in some ecological papers does not mean it travelled through the gut.

- Do not call guanine the only compound in droppings, all nitrogen “uric acid,” or nitrogen excretion the removal of every kind of bodily waste.
- Stored integument crystals are not excreta collected before defecation, and the *L. pallidus* crystal analysis does not identify every pale widow marking.
- Coxal glands are documented in the historical widow account, but no verified modern theridiid outlet map, secretion-rate assay or complete organ series was recovered; do not transfer tarantula gland-pair counts.
- No verified widow nephrocyte distribution, direct Malpighian transport measurement, complete dropping chemistry or standard daily defecation schedule was located.
- A pale spot beneath a web is not a species diagnosis or chemical identification; droplets can dry and mix with environmental material.
- The mesocosm study cannot isolate droppings from all prey-remain inputs, and increased soil ammonia is not proof that ammonia was the spider's main freshly excreted nitrogen compound.

**Suggested kicker:** Detecting a passenger does not tell us its job.

### TERMS

- **Microbiota / microbiome:** microbial organisms / their community and associated genetic context, with usage varying among papers.
- **Endosymbiont:** organism living within a host, often within cells; this term does not by itself mean beneficial.
- **16S amplicon:** bacterial marker-gene sequence used to infer taxonomic composition.
- **ASV:** amplicon sequence variant, not automatically a distinct bacterial species.
- **Relative abundance:** share of recovered reads; differs from prevalence, absolute cell number and metabolic activity.
- **Vertical transmission:** transmission from parent to offspring.
- **Phylosymbiosis:** resemblance between host-relatedness and microbiome-composition patterns, not proof of coevolution or nutritional dependence.

### Claims to avoid

- Do not title a half-abdomen or whole-animal dataset a resolved widow “gut microbiome”; intracellular symbionts, reproductive tissue and prey-derived signals can contribute.
- The population protocol included a week without food and surface sterilization, which reduces some confounds but does not dissect the intestinal lumen. [mowery2024]
- High prevalence and egg detection do not demonstrate digestive help, vitamin production, sex-ratio manipulation, cytoplasmic incompatibility or improved invasiveness.
- Amplicon assignment is not an isolated, functionally characterized strain; low-biomass tissue work needs negative controls, and RNA detections alone are not digestive-enzyme assays.
- Do not conflate Rickettsia, Rickettsiella and Rhabdochlamydia, or turn the genus-unresolved older report into a species-specific infection estimate.
- No verified widow antibiotic-curing/feeding-rescue experiment establishes a nutritional symbiosis; no gut-lumen atlas or microbial contribution to digestive energy yield was recovered.
- **web.guests** concerns visible web visitors, not these internal associations; venom-associated bacteria are not evidence that bacterial infection is the normal mechanism of widow feeding or biting.

- Show the actual sample types—whole spider, individual organs and half-abdomen—beside a clearly empty “gut-lumen localization still needed” window, keeping bacteria schematic and not to body scale.

### Search outcomes and publication gates

- Searches covered widow/theridiid digestive-fluid and midgut omics; mouth aperture and filter structure; pump musculature; lipid and glycogen storage; feeding/handling rates; starvation and respirometry; water permeability/drinking; excreta chemistry and coxal glands; gut microbiota and endosymbionts; and spiderling yolk/first meals. No absence claim in this dossier means a proof that no paper exists.
- A [USP dissertation deposited in October 2026](https://teses.usp.br/teses/disponiveis/87/87131/tde-29102026-090507/pt-br.html) was also checked: the landing page describes comparative digestive/venom work in six species but does not identify those six in its abstract. It was defended in 2022. It supplies no verified widow digestive dataset here; do not label its new deposit date a newly performed 2026 widow experiment.
- Public-release holds are explicit: old redback fasting extremes need complete treatment metadata; the brown-widow juvenile survival percentage needs its denominator and complete rearing conditions; the micron filter estimate stays comparative; adult widow aperture, volumetric intake and complete water/energy budgets are gaps.
- Recent primary work strengthened the hand-off beyond generic spider facts: widow mealworm processing and respiration, widow lipid carriers, an integument-crystal study including *L. pallidus*, brown-widow population microbiology, and widow-waste nutrient-transfer experiments are included with their actual sample types.
