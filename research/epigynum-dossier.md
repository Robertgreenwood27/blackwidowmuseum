### Priority qualifications for the current exhibits

1. Qualify the parent's storage claim: the existing ≥499-day L. hesperus anecdote remains HOLD because its original Kaston passage was not recovered; the earlier life dossier also left it unverified. Do not turn the supplied number into a newly checked fact. [kaston1970]
2. Replace any implication that the whole internal apparatus first appears at the final molt: late-subadult brown widows already possess cuticular ducts and sperm stores beneath the covering cuticle. [sentenska2021]
3. Do not animate an embolus continuing through the fertilisation duct to the uterus: insemination and later sperm release are different journeys. [berendonck2002; sentenska2025]
4. Preserve the spiralling-fold construction; an isolated closed hose is an inadequate reconstruction of the entire copulatory duct. [sentenska2021; berendonck2005]
5. Flag conflicting L. revivensis coil descriptions: 2002 reports two outward plus two returning coils; 2005 reports four plus two. Neither is a verified population range. [berendonck2002; berendonck2005]
6. Keep “uterus externus” attached to the widow authors' terminology: a later comparative study disputes homology and calls the egg-connected structure a secondary uterus externus. [zhan2019]
7. “At egg-laying” is a qualified functional account of fertilisation, not a directly filmed widow sperm–egg fusion event or proof of one universal spider fertilisation site. [berendonck2005; ouriel2016]
8. Pedicels attach developing oocytes; Ouriel did not demonstrate mature eggs passing through hollow stalks into the oviduct. [ouriel2016]
9. Female influence over access to two tracts is experimentally supported in redbacks; selective activation of a chosen male's sperm inside a store is not thereby demonstrated. [snow2005; berendonck2005]

## 0. body.epigynum — The Epigynum

**Suggested kicker:** Two routes into storage, another journey toward the eggs.

**Suggested summary:** A small plate beneath the abdomen opens into paired insemination pathways. Eleven halls follow the female system through maturation, coiled ducts, sperm storage, egg growth and laying, while distinguishing observed anatomy from proposed mechanisms of fertilisation and paternity control.

**Taxa studied:** LATRODECTUS: principally L. geometricus, L. revivensis, L. hasselti, L. hesperus and L. pallidus; historical American taxonomic comparisons are identified below. OTHER THERIDIID: Parasteatoda tepidariorum for a competing interpretation of distal reproductive anatomy. OTHER SPIDER and GENERAL SPIDER comparisons remain explicitly labelled.

### FIGURES

| Value | Unit | Short label | Qualifier | Sources |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2 | spermathecae | Paired sperm stores | Adult L. revivensis anatomy | [berendonck2002] |
| 2 | lobes per store | A narrow waist between lobes | L. geometricus; not two separate stores on each side | [sentenska2021] |
| Separate | incoming / outgoing sperm pathways | Entelegyne arrangement | Named widow anatomical accounts; distal terminology qualified in Hall 6 | [berendonck2002; sentenska2021] |

### OBSERVATIONS

- L. revivensis has a hardened genital plate immediately anterior to the epigastric furrow. **established — LATRODECTUS, adult anatomy** [berendonck2002]
- Brown-widow sperm stores can receive sperm before the final molt. **supported — LATRODECTUS, L. geometricus, late-subadult histology** [sentenska2021]
- Redback paternity depends partly on which storage organ each rival inseminates. **supported — LATRODECTUS, L. hasselti, manipulated matings** [snow2005]

These cards and observations orient the parent; the detailed anatomical distinctions belong in the halls.

### TERMS

- **Epigynum / epigyne:** external female genital structure, usually sclerotised in entelegyne spiders.
- **Atrium:** genital recess giving access to the copulatory openings.
- **Spermatheca:** sperm-storage organ; plural spermathecae.
- **Copulatory duct:** route used during insemination.
- **Fertilisation duct:** named outgoing sperm pathway from a spermatheca; distinct from the egg-carrying oviduct.

### Claims to avoid

- Two sperm stores do not mean two ovaries each permanently assigned to one father.
- “She keeps sperm alive for well over a year” needs a verified duration source before retaining it as unqualified parent copy.
- The epigynum is not an external egg sac, spinneret, respiratory slit or hourglass.
- A combined L. hesperus ovary / L. geometricus tract / L. revivensis muscle model must be labelled comparative.

### IMAGE BRIEFS

- **1536×1024 museum plate:** near-black background, cream linework; inverted adult female locator with the ventral epigynal region ahead of the hourglass enlarged into a species-labelled cutaway; paired stores remain visibly distinct from the ovaries.
- **1536×1024 museum plate:** two linked paths, incoming embolus and later outgoing sperm, with a separate egg pathway; use different line patterns, anatomical orientation marks and source-specific insets rather than one continuous insertion arrow.

### STAGE NOTES

- **Reference model:** use L. geometricus for the principal tract, based on 2021 Figs 1–9 and 2025 coupled-organ reconstructions; use a separately labelled L. hesperus ovary inset from Ouriel Figs 2–9. Whole-system placement is schematic rather than a measured reconstruction of one female. [sentenska2021; sentenska2025; ouriel2016]
- **Surface locator:** pedicel/body waist → anterior abdominal venter → epigynal plate and atrial access, with epigastric furrow immediately posterior; book-lung openings flank the furrow, and the hourglass lies farther posterior on the same venter. Use a whole-animal orientation indicator whenever the camera rotates. [berendonck2002; zhan2019]
- **Insertion layer:** atrium → selected copulatory opening → functional part of the spiralling copulatory duct → spermathecal entry. Brown-widow coupling is ipsilateral; the 2025 reconstruction reports the tip reaching the posterior lobe, whereas retained 2021 tips extend into the anterior lobe from its narrow entrance. Keep “inserted intact organ” and “retained fragment after withdrawal” as separate states. [sentenska2025; sentenska2021]
- **Storage/release layer:** sperm, rather than the embolus, proceeds from storage via the small fertilisation ducts to a common median pathway and the structure called uterus externus in the widow accounts. Label that terminology and add the Hall 6 qualification; do not graft a comparative homology proposal onto the widow reconstruction as settled anatomy. [berendonck2005; zhan2019]
- **Fold construction:** preserve connections between neighbouring duct folds and the narrow terminal guide; no artificial severing into separate spring turns. Use a reversible transparent surface, not independent rotating coils. [berendonck2002; sentenska2021]
- **Ovary layer:** depict oocytes projecting from the ovarian wall on pedicels, yolk accumulation and detached mature oocytes as separate stages; show the route into the oviduct as unresolved, with a dashed connection rather than an invented valve or hollow stalk. [ouriel2016]
- **Egg route:** ovarian region → oviduct/terminal genital tract → primary genital opening in the epigastric region → external egg mass; eggs never travel through the copulatory ducts or spermathecae. Distal “uterus” labels follow Hall 6's source convention; exact fusion site and egg-transit mechanics are not simulated. [ouriel2016; berendonck2005; zhan2019]
- **Plug positions:** separate toggles for narrow spermathecal entrance, a tip within a store and a fragment stranded in a copulatory duct; do not seal the entire atrium with a cork. Multiple fragments can occupy one side. [snow2006; segoli2008b]
- **Maturation toggle:** covering body cuticle present in the late subadult, locally opened by the male, then shed at adulthood while the internal storage system remains; do not animate stored sperm being discarded with an intact adult epigynum. [sentenska2021]
- **Muscle/flow overlay:** L. revivensis muscles connect epigynal plate and common fertilisation duct; proposed suction during laying is an interpretation, without measured pressure, flow speed or demonstrated left/right selection. No numerical fluid simulation is justified. [berendonck2005]

## 1. body.epigynum.the-plate — The Plate Under the Abdomen

**Suggested kicker:** A small entrance on the underside.

**Suggested summary:** The visible plate is the outer part of a much larger system. Its atrium and paired copulatory openings must be distinguished from the neighbouring furrow and the opening through which eggs leave.

**Taxa studied:** LATRODECTUS: L. revivensis and L. geometricus. GENERAL SPIDER: epigastric position and conventional entelegyne/haplogyne comparison.

### FIGURES

| Value | Unit | Short label | Qualifier | Sources |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2 | copulatory openings | One on each atrial side | Adult L. revivensis | [berendonck2002] |
| ≈30 | µm, plate cuticle thickness | Hardened surface | L. revivensis histology; thickness, not plate width | [berendonck2005] |
| 1 versus 2 | sperm-route types per store | Shared versus separate entry/exit | Conventional haplogyne / entelegyne distinction; anatomical diversity requires qualification | [zhan2019] |

### OBSERVATIONS

- The adult L. revivensis plate is arched and transversely oval. **established — LATRODECTUS, microscopy** [berendonck2002]
- Its atrial aperture is approximately triangular. **established — LATRODECTUS, L. revivensis, ventral view** [berendonck2002]
- Adult brown widows expose a slit-like atrial access. **established — LATRODECTUS, L. geometricus** [sentenska2021]
- Hairs overhang the L. revivensis atrial entrance. **established — LATRODECTUS, surface anatomy** [berendonck2002]
- Brown-widow coupling contacts the caudal epigynal lip. **supported — LATRODECTUS, L. geometricus, cryofixed mating pairs** [sentenska2025]
- The epigastric furrow lies between the paired book-lung spiracles. **established — GENERAL SPIDER, comparative ventral anatomy** [zhan2019]

### TERMS

- **Ventral:** belonging to the underside, regardless of gravity.
- **Epigastric furrow:** transverse integumental fold near the front of the abdominal venter.
- **Sclerotisation:** hardening of cuticle; pigmentation alone does not measure its degree.
- **Entelegyne:** conventional genital arrangement with distinct incoming and outgoing sperm routes.
- **Haplogyne:** conventional arrangement in which a storage duct carries sperm both ways; not simply “a spider without a plate.”

### Claims to avoid

- Do not substitute the ≈30-µm cuticle thickness or a 0.1-mm illustration scale bar for epigynal width; a comparable widow plate-size range remains HOLD.
- “Between the lung slits” is a locator, not a claim that the atrium opens into a lung.
- A triangular L. revivensis aperture and slit-like L. geometricus access are source-specific views, not contradictory universal silhouettes.
- Signpost body.breathing and body.hourglass for the neighbouring organs; body.pedipalps.fit owns the male clamp mechanism.

### IMAGE BRIEFS

- **1536×1024 museum plate:** near-black background and cream linework; ventral locator with body waist, epigynal plate, flanking lung slits and posterior hourglass; separate L. revivensis and L. geometricus aperture insets, each with view and source.

## 2. body.epigynum.becoming-adult — Opening at the Last Molt

**Suggested kicker:** Covered does not mean absent.

**Suggested summary:** Internal genital development precedes its public unveiling. A late-subadult widow can already have functioning sperm stores beneath cuticle that a male must open to reach the normal insemination route.

**Taxa studied:** LATRODECTUS: L. geometricus, direct developmental series; L. hasselti and L. geometricus, immature-mating reproductive outcomes. Historical 1962 development is a secondary locator here, not an independently inspected plate series.

### FIGURES

| Value | Unit | Short label | Qualifier | Sources |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | molt remaining | Penultimate female | Stage definition for late-subadult mating | [sentenska2021] |
| ≈6 | days before adulthood | Darkening beneath the covering | L. geometricus external series; rearing-dependent observation | [sentenska2021] |
| 15 / 15 | shed cuticles inspected | Virgin / immature-mated | L. geometricus final-molt inspection, not a fertility sample | [sentenska2021] |

### OBSERVATIONS

- Early-subadult brown-widow genital structures are outlined by epithelium before their cuticular components form. **supported — LATRODECTUS, L. geometricus, sections** [sentenska2021]
- Late-subadult females retain a covering over the epigynum. **established — LATRODECTUS, L. geometricus** [sentenska2021]
- Males open that covering to expose the normal copulatory entrances. **supported — LATRODECTUS, L. geometricus, observed immature mating** [sentenska2021]
- Brown-widow internal genital structures remain through the final molt. **supported — LATRODECTUS, L. geometricus, exuviae and anatomy** [sentenska2021]
- Immature matings can yield viable offspring after adulthood. **supported — LATRODECTUS, L. hasselti and L. geometricus, mating study** [biaggio2016]
- The coupled brown-widow mechanism was similar across adult and immature matings. **supported — LATRODECTUS, L. geometricus, micro-CT** [sentenska2025]

### TERMS

- **Penultimate / subadult:** the instar immediately before adulthood.
- **Late subadult:** later part of that instar, not a separate universal numbered instar.
- **Exuvia:** shed cuticle; plural exuviae.
- **Invagination:** inward folding of an epithelial surface during development.
- **Immature mating:** insemination before the final molt, distinguished from mating just after it.

### Claims to avoid

- Cuticle opening does not establish sperm injection into hemolymph; the brown-widow study supports the normal duct route.
- Do not say “genitalia form only at the final molt,” or that any juvenile female can store sperm.
- An immature-mated female's future fertility does not quantify how many sperm survive the molt; a retention percentage remains HOLD.
- Signpost body.pedipalps.development for the male timetable and life.molting / life.development for ecdysis and growth.

### IMAGE BRIEFS

- **1536×1024 museum plate:** near-black with cream linework; early subadult, covered late subadult, locally opened late subadult and adult genital regions in matched ventral orientation; the sperm-bearing internal structures persist across the last transition.

## 3. body.epigynum.copulatory-ducts — The Coiled Path

**Suggested kicker:** Follow the fold, then the return.

**Suggested summary:** A duct outline can hide the connections between its coils. Sectioning reveals a folded cuticular system with a narrow terminal guide, while conflicting published counts warn against treating every visible loop as a standard turn.

**Taxa studied:** LATRODECTUS: L. revivensis, cleared and sectioned anatomy; L. geometricus, folded ducts and coupled organs; historical American female comparisons in McCrone and Levi.

### FIGURES

| Value | Unit | Short label | Qualifier | Sources |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2 + 2 | described coils | Outward course and return | L. revivensis, 2002 wording; conflicts with 2005, not a species range | [berendonck2002] |
| 4 + 2 | described coils | A later count | L. revivensis, 2005 wording and Fig. 5; counting discrepancy unresolved | [berendonck2005] |
| ≤17 | µm, lateral tube width | Narrow terminal guide | L. revivensis sectioned entrance; not total duct diameter or length | [berendonck2002] |

### OBSERVATIONS

- Each L. revivensis copulatory duct turns back toward its spermatheca. **established — LATRODECTUS, traced anatomy** [berendonck2002]
- Its terminal duct flattens beside a more strongly sclerotised guide. **established — LATRODECTUS, L. revivensis** [berendonck2002]
- Brown-widow sections reveal connected spiral folds rather than wholly isolated tubes. **established — LATRODECTUS, L. geometricus** [sentenska2021]
- The male brown-widow embolus follows the copulatory duct during insertion. **established — LATRODECTUS, L. geometricus, in-copula reconstruction** [sentenska2025]
- McCrone and Levi described an extra female duct loop in L. mactans relative to their curacaviensis-group comparison. **supported — LATRODECTUS, historical diagnosis** [mccrone1964]

### TERMS

- **Duct lumen:** internal space within the functional passage.
- **Spiralling fold:** coiled cuticular folding with connections between adjacent portions.
- **Sclerotised guide:** reinforced terminal passage accommodating the embolus.
- **Reversal:** change in the direction of a traced duct's course.
- **Coil count:** count under a stated tracing and viewing convention; not a length measurement.

### Claims to avoid

- HOLD a standardized total duct length, coil pitch and genus-wide female loop-count table.
- Do not average the conflicting 2002/2005 counts, label them individual variation, or silently select one as the true species count.
- A correspondence with male coiling does not establish identical rigid helices or a measured one-to-one relation of total path lengths.
- Cross-reference body.pedipalps.the-coil and body.pedipalps.fit; those halls retain male turn counts and coupling contacts.

### IMAGE BRIEFS

- **1536×1024 museum plate:** near-black and cream linework; reference-based L. geometricus duct surface beside an interpretive section exposing connecting folds; trace the functional passage without manufacturing isolated tubing.
- **1536×1024 museum plate:** L. revivensis terminal guide and sharp return enlarged, with the two published counting descriptions boxed as an unresolved comparison rather than merged into a new drawing.

## 4. body.epigynum.spermathecae — Two Sperm Stores

**Suggested kicker:** Paired chambers with living tissue around them.

**Suggested summary:** Each store contains two rounded lobes joined by a waist. Its cuticular wall is surrounded by epithelium and glandular units, and the left and right routes can receive different ejaculates.

**Taxa studied:** LATRODECTUS: L. revivensis, fine structure; L. geometricus, maturation; L. hasselti, separately manipulated insemination routes.

### FIGURES

| Value | Unit | Short label | Qualifier | Sources |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2 × 2 | stores × lobes | Paired dumbbells | L. revivensis adult genital anatomy | [berendonck2002] |
| 30–45 | µm, cuticle thickness | Thick storage wall | L. revivensis descriptive histology; not lumen diameter | [berendonck2005] |
| ≤40 | µm, pore diameter | Openings through the wall | L. revivensis anterior/posterior lobes, 2002 surface description; not gland-cell width | [berendonck2002] |

### OBSERVATIONS

- A narrow middle region joins the two L. revivensis lobes. **established — LATRODECTUS, microscopy** [berendonck2002]
- Its incoming and outgoing sperm passages meet different regions of the store. **established — LATRODECTUS, L. revivensis** [berendonck2002]
- Glandular units communicate through pores in its cuticular wall. **established — LATRODECTUS, L. revivensis, sections** [berendonck2005]
- Its narrow middle region lacks the lobe's glandular units. **supported — LATRODECTUS, L. revivensis, sampled histology** [berendonck2005]
- Adult brown-widow storage cuticle is thicker than late-subadult storage cuticle. **supported — LATRODECTUS, L. geometricus, stage comparison** [sentenska2021]
- Redback rivals can inseminate opposite stores. **supported — LATRODECTUS, L. hasselti, controlled matings** [snow2005]

### TERMS

- **Lobe:** rounded subdivision within one storage organ.
- **Epithelium:** tissue lining or covering a surface.
- **Glandular unit:** secretory cellular assembly associated with an outlet.
- **Pore canal:** passage across cuticle, distinct from the organ's main lumen.
- **Paired independence:** separate insemination access; not proof of independently selectable sperm release.

### Claims to avoid

- HOLD a calibrated whole-store volume, sperm capacity and consistent lobe-size range; source scale bars and section areas cannot supply these automatically.
- Do not depict a bare glass reservoir: the sclerotised wall and surrounding living tissues are different components.
- Separate left/right entry does not prove a female can activate or empty either side at will.
- Cross-reference body.pedipalps.two-palps for the male sequence; this hall owns the receiving structures.

### IMAGE BRIEFS

- **1536×1024 museum plate:** near-black with cream linework; two source-based L. revivensis stores, one intact and one sectioned through a lobe, with gland outlets, cuticle and lumen visually distinct; mark anatomical left/right and separate magnifications.

## 5. body.epigynum.storage — Sperm Kept Alive

**Suggested kicker:** Storage is a changing cellular state.

**Suggested summary:** Widow sperm can remain available for successive broods. Microscopy distinguishes encapsulated cells, cells that have lost their capsules and fully activated sperm; those stages should not be collapsed into a single switch controlled by the female.

**Taxa studied:** LATRODECTUS: L. revivensis, sperm histology through reproductive stages; L. hasselti, reproduction after a feeding interruption. L. hesperus is retained as an explicit duration-verification gap.

### FIGURES

| Value | Unit | Short label | Qualifier | Sources |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ≈24 | hours after mating | Decapsulation already detectable | L. revivensis sampled females; not a universal activation deadline | [berendonck2005] |
| ≥4 | viable egg sacs already produced | Stored sperm remained | Single-mated laboratory L. revivensis examined anatomically; no duration inferred | [berendonck2005] |
| 6 | weeks of dietary treatment | Reproduction can pause | L. hasselti experiment; later production after refeeding, not maximum storage duration | [stoltz2010b] |

### OBSERVATIONS

- Recently mated L. revivensis contain encapsulated sperm. **established — LATRODECTUS, histology** [berendonck2005]
- Some decapsulated cells remain coiled. **established — LATRODECTUS, L. revivensis, fixed preparations** [berendonck2005]
- Encapsulated cells persisted after several viable broods. **supported — LATRODECTUS, L. revivensis, single-mated laboratory females** [berendonck2005]
- Mated redbacks resumed sac production after food restriction ended. **supported — LATRODECTUS, L. hasselti, feeding experiment** [stoltz2010b]
- Brown-widow storage secretions differ in staining between subadult and adult stages. **supported — LATRODECTUS, L. geometricus, histochemistry** [sentenska2021]

### TERMS

- **Encapsulation:** a secretion sheath surrounding a sperm cell.
- **Decapsulation:** loss of that sheath; not necessarily uncoiling or motility.
- **Activation:** transition to a functional fertilising state; its criteria must be specified.
- **Viability:** capacity to remain functional, not established merely by an intact-looking cell.
- **Cryptic female choice:** female-mediated bias in fertilisation after mating; requires evidence distinguishing it from sperm competition and male effects.

### Claims to avoid

- **HOLD ≥499 days:** supplied as one L. hesperus female with unknown mating date; original Kaston passage was inaccessible in this pass, so neither the exact lower bound nor the endpoint is newly verified. Keep it out of released FIGURES until the original account is checked. [kaston1970]
- “Nourishment” is plausible but no widow-specific nutrient recipe, oxygen regime or experimentally demonstrated metabolic support pathway was verified.
- Berendonck and Greven's low-choice interpretation concerns selective sperm manipulation in L. revivensis; it does not exclude behavioural influence on which males inseminate which tract.
- No demonstrated species-wide storage maximum, universal sperm lifespan or selective activation of a chosen male's cells is proposed.
- Cross-reference body.pedipalps.sperm-induction for sperm before transfer and life.egg-sac for repeated broods.

### IMAGE BRIEFS

- **1536×1024 museum plate:** near-black and cream linework; L. revivensis encapsulated/coiled and decapsulated/still-coiled cells in separate source-based insets; any fully motile state is labelled unresolved rather than shown swimming toward an egg on a measured schedule.

## 6. body.epigynum.fertilisation — Two Routes, One Meeting

**Suggested kicker:** The sperm's exit is not its entrance.

**Suggested summary:** Small fertilisation pathways leave the stores and converge medially in the widow accounts. Their connection with the egg route is described through a contested anatomical vocabulary, so the exhibit should distinguish observed connections from the precise site and mechanics of fertilisation.

**Taxa studied:** LATRODECTUS: L. revivensis and L. geometricus. OTHER THERIDIID: Parasteatoda tepidariorum; OTHER SPIDER: Diphya wulingensis in the 2019 reconstruction, whose caption prints “wulingensi.” GENERAL SPIDER: competing interpretations of distal reproductive homology.

### FIGURES

| Value | Unit | Short label | Qualifier | Sources |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2 → 1 | small ducts → common pathway | Paired exits converge | L. revivensis; “common fertilisation duct” in the source | [berendonck2005] |
| Posterior lobe | outlet region | Exit differs from entry | L. revivensis spermathecal anatomy | [berendonck2002] |
| 121 | species in comparative survey | A broader anatomical challenge | 147 epigyna, 34 families; not 121 widows | [zhan2019] |

### OBSERVATIONS

- L. revivensis fertilisation ducts originate in the posterior spermathecal wall. **established — LATRODECTUS, sectioned anatomy** [berendonck2002]
- Its two small ducts feed a common median duct. **established — LATRODECTUS, L. revivensis** [berendonck2005]
- Brown-widow sections likewise show a common fertilisation pathway. **established — LATRODECTUS, L. geometricus** [sentenska2021]
- Muscle fibres join the L. revivensis common duct to the plate. **established — LATRODECTUS, histology** [berendonck2005]
- Zhan and colleagues connected the oviduct to a secondary uterus externus in Parasteatoda. **supported — OTHER THERIDIID, P. tepidariorum, three-dimensional reconstruction** [zhan2019]
- Their comparative interpretation challenges the traditional uterus-externus homology. **debated — GENERAL SPIDER, anatomical reinterpretation** [zhan2019]

### TERMS

- **Fertilisation duct:** outgoing sperm pathway from a store.
- **Common fertilisation duct:** median convergence named in the widow accounts.
- **Oviduct:** egg-carrying passage, distinct from a sperm-storage duct.
- **Uterus externus:** source-dependent name for a distal reproductive structure; homology must be specified.
- **Homology:** correspondence interpreted as shared evolutionary origin, not merely similar position.

### Claims to avoid

- Do not state that the embolus enters the uterus or ovary through the fertilisation duct.
- Release near oviposition and fertilisation as eggs traverse the terminal tract are the widow authors' functional model; direct widow fusion timing and exact location remain HOLD.
- Proposed suction by the plate–duct muscle is an inference, not a measured pump cycle or demonstrated selective withdrawal from one store.
- Do not transfer Parasteatoda's full reconstruction to Latrodectus, or treat the 2019 terminology as a new widow dissection.
- Signpost Hall 7 for egg formation, Hall 8 for extrusion and life.egg-sac for the brood outside the body.

### IMAGE BRIEFS

- **1536×1024 museum plate:** near-black and cream linework; L. revivensis sperm-entry and sperm-exit routes in contrasting line patterns, common duct and source-labelled uterus; a smaller boxed OTHER THERIDIID comparison explains the alternate terminology without fusing the two models.

## 7. body.epigynum.ovaries — Making Eggs

**Suggested kicker:** A growing egg remains attached by a stalk.

**Suggested summary:** Oocytes grow on the ovarian wall and accumulate yolk before laying. Western-widow microscopy describes the cellular sequence; feeding experiments show why repeated egg production cannot be treated as a fixed clock.

**Taxa studied:** LATRODECTUS: L. hesperus in Ouriel's MSc thesis; L. hasselti in a controlled feeding experiment. Thesis results are primary observations, with their limited sampling and unresolved transport model retained.

### OBSERVATIONS

## 10. body.epigynum.female-control — Her Side of Mating

**Suggested kicker:** Access to the second store changes the outcome.

**Suggested summary:** Female behaviour can determine whether a male inseminates one route or both. Redback experiments connect that paired anatomy to paternity, while widow histology places limits on claims of choosing sperm inside an already filled store.

**Taxa studied:** LATRODECTUS: L. hasselti, experimentally assigned storage routes and mating behaviour; L. revivensis, proposed limits on sperm manipulation; L. geometricus, coupling mechanism. No genus-wide polyandry percentage is proposed.

### FIGURES

| Value | Unit | Short label | Qualifier | Sources |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 79.8 / 49.3 | %, mean first-male paternity | Same / opposite storage organ | L. hasselti manipulated rival matings; medians 89.4 / 49.9%; treatment results, not field rates | [snow2005] |
| 38 / 10 | %, first males with complete paternity | Same / opposite organ treatment | L. hasselti; “complete” refers to scored offspring, not all future sacs | [snow2005] |
| 2 | separately accessible stores | Opportunity for route control | L. hasselti; anatomical basis of experimental comparison | [snow2005] |

### OBSERVATIONS

- Redback rivals forced into one store produced stronger first-male precedence. **supported — LATRODECTUS, L. hasselti, manipulation** [snow2005]
- Opposite-store matings produced approximately equal mean paternity. **supported — LATRODECTUS, L. hasselti, experimental treatment** [snow2005]
- Redback males avoided previously inseminated routes. **supported — LATRODECTUS, L. hasselti, experiment** [snow2005]
- Fatal first-copulation cannibalism can prevent access to the other store. **supported — LATRODECTUS, L. hasselti, behavioural interpretation** [snow2005]
- L. revivensis anatomy was interpreted as offering little scope for selective sperm manipulation. **supported — LATRODECTUS, anatomical inference rather than choice assay** [berendonck2005]
- Brown-widow somersaulting did not change the basic coupling mechanism. **supported — LATRODECTUS, L. geometricus, reconstructed matings** [sentenska2025]

### TERMS

- **Polyandry:** mating with more than one male.
- **Sperm precedence:** disproportionate paternity associated with mating order under specified conditions.
- **Paternity share:** fraction of scored offspring assigned to a sire.
- **Rejection:** behaviour preventing or terminating mating; does not identify an internal sperm-selection mechanism.
- **Route access:** opportunity to inseminate a specified tract, distinct from later use of its sperm.

### Claims to avoid

- “She chooses the father” overstates evidence unless the mechanism and experimental conditions are named.
- Equal treatment means do not guarantee exactly 50:50 paternity in each female; same-store results do not establish a universal first-male rule.
- HOLD a comparable genus-wide field remating rate and a quantitative female-only rejection or copulation-termination rate; tip counts and laboratory mating opportunities are different measures.
- Do not turn an absence of internal sensilla in sampled L. revivensis regions into a claim that females cannot sense courtship or influence mating.
- Signpost life.courtship, life.cannibalism and life.mating-plugs for full behavioural sequences and adaptive explanations; body.pedipalps.two-palps retains the male's second-palp opportunity.

### IMAGE BRIEFS

- **1536×1024 museum plate:** near-black and cream linework; redback paired-store map with rival ejaculates assigned to the same versus opposite store in two panels, labelled experimental means; female silhouettes indicate first- versus second-route access without implying conscious sperm sorting.
