### Priority qualifications for the current exhibit

1. **Retain the one-pair and one-posterior-opening cards.** The Theridiidae diagnosis explicitly supports two book lungs and a fairly wide tracheal slit just before the spinnerets; this is family-level anatomical evidence, not a modern Latrodectus reconstruction of every internal tube. [jocque2006]
2. **Retain the epigastric location, with a precise locator.** The two lung slits occupy the lateral ends of the epigastric furrow; the adult female genital plate lies between them, anterior to the furrow, rather than being an entrance into the lungs. [snodgrass1952; berendonck2002]
3. **Clarify the “pages” sentence and illustration.** Alternating air and hemolymph compartments are supported; add their separate supporting structures and label which compartment is shown, because historical and modern accounts use lamellar terminology differently. [kuntzel2019]
4. **Refine “returns toward the heart through pulmonary vessels.”** The pulmonary return enters the pericardial sinus surrounding the heart, then the heart through ostia; do not draw pulmonary veins entering a vertebrate-like atrium or closed capillary circuit. [snodgrass1952]
5. **Keep hemocyanin, but distinguish levels of evidence.** General spider transport is established, and L. mirabilis has direct hemolymph biochemical evidence; an exact widow oligomer, oxygen affinity or saturation curve is not supplied here. [foelix2011; cunningham2000; rehm2012]
6. **Flag the current definition “a stack of thin, leaf-like air chambers” as incomplete, not false.** It must not lead an illustrator to omit hemolymph channels, epithelial supports or the cuticular air-side lining. [kuntzel2019]
7. **Do not add a universal “lungs became tubes once” history.** Modern phylogenomic reconstruction infers repeated transformations, and median tracheae have an apodemal history distinct from lateral lung-derived tubes. [ramirez2000; ramirez2021]
8. **Do not add “widows hold their breath underwater for a day.”** The direct western-widow study manipulated atmospheric oxygen, not submersion; survival depended on both oxygen and temperature. [liu2008]
9. **Keep the existing generated plate review pending.** Its alt text cannot establish species-specific lamellar counts, wall dimensions, tube routes, or effective tidal ventilation; no such widow calibration was recovered.

## 0. body.breathing — Book Lungs

**Suggested kicker:** Two kinds of air space, one living animal.

**Suggested summary:** A widow has paired book lungs near the front underside of her abdomen and a second opening feeding tracheal tubes near the spinnerets. Eleven halls follow the respiratory surfaces, oxygen transport and changing demands of rest, hunger, activity, temperature and growth.

**Taxa studied:** LATRODECTUS: L. mactans as reported in digestive respirometry; L. hasselti in female fasting and male courtship studies; L. hesperus in low-oxygen and water-barrier experiments; L. mirabilis hemolymph biochemistry. ## 1. body.breathing.two-systems — Lungs and Tubes

**Suggested kicker:** A shared starting plan, several evolutionary routes.

**Suggested summary:** Widows combine anterior book lungs with posterior tracheae. That arrangement sits within a much wider spider history of retained lungs, transformed lungs, apodemal tubes and losses, rather than a single ladder from primitive to advanced breathing.

**Taxa studied:** LATRODECTUS: family-level Theridiidae diagnosis, not new dissection. GENERAL SPIDER: mesothele, mygalomorph and araneomorph comparisons. OTHER SPIDER: respiratory character reconstruction across sampled araneomorph lineages.

### FIGURES

| Value | Unit | Short label | Qualifier | Sources |

| 1 + tracheae | lung pair + tube system | The widow arrangement | Theridiidae diagnosis; no partition of actual oxygen uptake implied | [jocque2006] |
| 6 | inferred transformations | Posterior lungs to similar tracheae | Ramírez et al. character-evolution reconstruction, not six changes inside Latrodectus | [ramirez2021] |

### OBSERVATIONS

- Theridiidae have two anterior book lungs and a posterior tracheal opening. **established — LATRODECTUS, family-level anatomy** [jocque2006]
- Mesotheles and mygalomorphs retain the ancestral two-pair lung arrangement. **established — GENERAL SPIDER, comparative respiratory anatomy** [lopardo2022]
- Some araneomorph lineages retain two lung pairs, so one pair is not universal for that suborder. **established — OTHER SPIDER, hypochilid comparison** [snodgrass1952]
- Lateral posterior tracheae are interpreted as modifications of the posterior lung pair. **supported — GENERAL SPIDER, comparative morphology and ontogeny** [ramirez2000]
- Median posterior tracheae are interpreted as modified entapophyses of the same segment. **supported — GENERAL SPIDER, anatomical homology analysis** [ramirez2000]
- Modern phylogenomic mapping supports repeated origins of similar posterior tracheal systems. **supported — OTHER SPIDER, sampled araneomorph phylogeny** [ramirez2021]

### TERMS

- **Araneomorph:** member of the large spider suborder containing widows.
- **Mesothele:** member of the earliest-diverging living spider lineage.
- **Mygalomorph:** member of the lineage containing tarantulas and their relatives.
- **Bimodal respiration:** gas exchange using two kinds of respiratory organ.
- **Homology:** correspondence attributed to shared evolutionary origin.
- **Entapophysis:** inward skeletal projection involved in the median-tube homology argument.

### Claims to avoid

- “Every araneomorph has one lung pair,” “all tracheae are rolled-up lung pages,” and “the transformation happened only once.”
- The six inferred events depend on a particular tree and character model; they are not directly observed evolutionary episodes.
- The proposed energetic connection with aerial-web spinning is a testable hypothesis, not a demonstrated cause of widow anatomy. [ramirez2021]
- Having both systems does not establish a 50:50 gas-exchange split or two alternating breathing modes.
- Signpost genus.family-tree and life.ballooning for taxonomic dates and dispersal; this hall owns respiratory character changes.

### IMAGE BRIEFS

- **1536×1024 museum plate:** near-black background, cream linework; three independently labelled abdominal plans—mesothele/mygalomorph two-pair condition, widow one-pair-plus-tubes condition, and a tracheae-only comparator—with no evolutionary superiority ladder.
- **1536×1024 museum plate:** simplified comparative branching tree with repeated lung-to-tube changes, lateral and median origins shown separately; no geological dates or branch widths inferred from the diagram.

- **Hypercapnia:** elevated carbon dioxide availability.
- **Ventilation:** bulk movement of respiratory medium, distinct from diffusion.

## 5. body.breathing.exchange — Across the Thin Wall

### TERMS

- **Partial-pressure gradient:** difference in a gas's partial pressure driving diffusion.
- **Hemocyanin:** extracellular copper-containing oxygen carrier.
- **Hexamer:** molecular assembly of six subunits.
- **Oxygen affinity:** tendency of a carrier to bind oxygen under specified conditions.
- **P50:** oxygen partial pressure at half-saturation under a defined assay.
- **Pericardial sinus:** hemolymph space surrounding the heart.

- HOLD a Latrodectus P50, cooperativity coefficient, hemocyanin concentration or temperature/pH-dependent binding curve.

- Signpost body.circulation for the heart, ostia and open circuit; keep this hall at the exchange/carrier interface.

### TERMS

- **Standard metabolic rate:** baseline metabolic rate under specified resting, postabsorptive conditions.
- **Respirometry:** estimation of physiological expenditure through respiratory gas measurements.
- **Whole-animal rate:** gas exchange per animal per unit time.
- **Mass-specific rate:** gas exchange divided by body mass.
- **Allometry:** a size relationship departing from simple proportionality.
- **STPD:** standard temperature and pressure, dry, for reporting gas volume.

### TERMS

- **Hypoxia tolerance:** ability to survive a defined reduction in available oxygen.
- **Anoxia:** absence of available oxygen; distinct from 2% O₂.

- **LT50:** estimated exposure duration associated with 50% mortality under stated conditions.

Methods specify 120 females of each species, whereas the abstract's wording is less explicit; use the Methods denominator. The recovery-inclusive LT50 is estimated, and metabolic mechanism was proposed for future testing. [petillon2009]
