Researched 23 November 2026. Scope: BWM-01.16, body.silk-glands, proposed as a parent case containing ten halls. Existing parent id and accession are preserved; hall ids, titles, kickers and summaries are suggestions. This is the single-file research hand-off; neither exhibit YAML nor the source registry is edited.

## Reading this dossier

- Bracketed citation keys refer to content/sources.yaml or the NEW entries in SOURCES below. The complete hourglass dossier, source registry, silk-glands and spinnerets cases, and all current web exhibits, including their nested cases, were read before drafting. Registry presence alone was not treated as verification.
- **established** = directly documented and secure as qualified; **supported** = a particular experiment, limited sample or interpretation; **debated** = an unresolved mechanism or explanation. Confidence belongs to the qualified sentence, not automatically to a whole genus.
- Evidence classes are **LATRODECTUS**, **OTHER THERIDIID**, **OTHER SPIDER**, and **GENERAL SPIDER**. A recombinant protein based on a widow sequence supplies LATRODECTUS molecular evidence, not direct observation inside a living gland; that distinction is stated wherever relevant.
- FIGURES are candidate fact cards, not illustrations. Gland classes, individual organs, spigots, transcripts, gene loci and detected proteins are different counts. Means, errors, samples and experimental conditions remain attached to measurements.
- The anatomical reference animal is an adult female *L. hesperus*. Outlet descriptions also use *L. variolus* and the specimens identified as *L. mactans* in the original microscopy study; these are separately labelled rather than silently pooled or taxonomically reassigned.
- Primary microscopy, sequencing, protein identification and assembly experiments underpin the claims. Accessible indexed primary-paper passages are identified in the verification notes where a complete article could not be retrieved. Unverified details are recorded as gaps, not filled with a generic orb-weaver diagram.
- A gland's **tail** is secretory tissue, its **sac/ampulla** contains a lumen, and its **duct** leads to a **spigot** on a **spinneret**. This three-part vocabulary fits ampullate glands particularly well; it must not be imposed on every gland class.
- This case owns the factory: anatomy, secretory biology, storage, assembly and regulation. Product uses receive only enough context to explain a gland, followed by explicit links to the Silk & Web vault.
- All widow scenes must show tangle cobwebs, with substrate-attached gumfoot lines where relevant, never orb webs. Comparator spiders and experimental diagrams must be clearly separated from widow anatomy. Internal false colours are explanatory, not observed organ colours.

### Priority qualifications for the current exhibits

1. Replace the parent kicker “Seven glands, seven kinds of silk”: seven **classes** include numerous individual glands, and aggregate secretions are adhesive rather than a seventh dry thread.
2. Preserve the adult-female and species qualification on the seven-class inventory; the adult male apparatus differs.
3. Do not imply one gland class, one gene, one product: expression overlaps and finished silks contain mixtures.
4. Retain both aggregate pairs and the corrected 2018 identifications. [chaw2018]
5. Separate flagelliform outlet identification from unresolved product function. [coddington1989; chaw2018]
6. Revise body.spinnerets' emphasis: processing begins before the spigot. [casem2002; xu2015]
7. Keep the familiar dragline mechanics in web.dragline; only an orientation card is warranted here, not a second strength-comparison exhibit.
8. Replace any fixed “refills in X hours,” “regrows every gland at each molt,” or “recycles 90% of its silk” claim with the specific evidence and limits below.

## 0. body.silk-glands — The Silk Factory

**Suggested kicker:** Many organs, overlapping recipes.

**Suggested summary:** Silk begins in living tissue: cells make proteins, glands store concentrated dope, and ducts help assemble the outgoing material. Ten halls follow the organs and the evidence that connects a gland, a gene and a finished thread.

**Taxa studied:** LATRODECTUS: principally female *L. hesperus* for these additional overview items.

### FIGURES

| 11.8 | kDa, predicted | A small egg-case component | ECP3 sequence prediction, not a whole-fibre mass | [shanafelt2021] |

### OBSERVATIONS

- The major ampullate sac adds material around the product arriving from the tail. **supported — LATRODECTUS, L. hesperus, electron microscopy** [casem2002]
- Acidification assembled fibres from excised widow major ampullate dope in vitro. **supported — LATRODECTUS, L. hesperus, laboratory assembly** [xu2015]
- Widow AcSp1 domains use an assembly behaviour different from the familiar dragline N-terminal switch. **supported — LATRODECTUS, L. hesperus sequence, recombinant experiments** [qin2025]

These two cards and three observations are five additions to the parent, not a request to reproduce every hall's detail there.

### TERMS

- **Secretory epithelium:** a sheet of cells that produces and releases material.
- **Lumen:** the space inside a gland or duct.
- **Spinning dope:** the concentrated fluid precursor of silk.
- **Spidroin:** a member of the spider silk protein family; not a name for every molecule found in silk.

### Claims to avoid

- Seven types does not mean seven organs, seven exclusive proteins or seven known widow fibres.
- A protein detected in gland tissue may maintain the cells rather than become an exported silk ingredient.

### IMAGE BRIEFS

- Make an adult female western-widow abdominal cutaway with a locator for the ten halls; preserve space for gut, ovaries and other organs rather than filling the abdomen with seven equally sized balloons.
- Use a gland → dope → outlet → fibre plate with separate scales and anatomical directions; label magnified cellular and molecular insets as schematics.

## 1. body.silk-glands.major-ampullate — The Gland Behind the Lifeline

**Suggested kicker:** The storage sac also contributes to the recipe.

**Suggested summary:** The best-studied widow gland combines protein secretion with downstream processing. Its tissue is more elaborate than a passive reservoir attached to a nozzle; finished-thread performance belongs in web.dragline.

**Taxa studied:** LATRODECTUS: female *L. hesperus* tissue and fibres; *L. variolus* outlet morphology.

### OBSERVATIONS

- The major ampullate tail is specialized for substantial protein synthesis. **established — LATRODECTUS, L. hesperus** [casem2002]
- Its ampulla stores the silk precursor. **established — LATRODECTUS, L. hesperus** [casem2002]
- Electron-dense sac secretion was interpreted as an outer coating around the tail-derived core. **supported — LATRODECTUS, L. hesperus** [casem2002]
- One duct cell type contains vesicles with a honeycomb appearance. **established — LATRODECTUS, L. hesperus** [casem2002]
- Another duct cell type bears elaborate apical microvilli. **established — LATRODECTUS, L. hesperus** [casem2002]
- MaSp1 and MaSp2 are major structural ingredients of widow dragline. **established — LATRODECTUS, L. hesperus** [ayoub2007]
- Proteomics detected AcSp1 and cysteine-rich proteins in dragline material. **supported — LATRODECTUS, L. hesperus, recovered protein mixture** [larracas2016]
- Carboxylic ester hydrolase, a lectin candidate and mucin-related protein were among proteins detected in both gland and fibre. **supported — LATRODECTUS, L. hesperus, proteomic identifications** [chaw2015]
- In northern-widow preparations, the major ampullate outlet lies on the anterior lateral spinneret beside pyriform outlets. **established — LATRODECTUS, L. variolus** [coddington1989]
- Major ampullate silk supplies lifelines and structural web threads. **established — LATRODECTUS, L. hesperus** [blackledge2005]

### TERMS

- **Ampulla:** the expanded sac region of an ampullate gland.
- **Microvilli:** small projections increasing a cell's exposed surface area.
- **Apical:** facing the gland lumen in this context.
- **CRP:** cysteine-rich protein; a non-spidroin protein category in these studies.

### Claims to avoid

- The honeycomb vesicles' function was unknown; microvilli support a water-reabsorption interpretation, not a measured widow water flux. [casem2002]
- Do not equate an electron-dense coating with a chemically identified lipid, named protein or antimicrobial layer.
- No single standard gland volume or native dope inventory was verified; use actual micrograph scale bars, not a fabricated millimetre average.
- CRPs and other associated proteins are candidates for assembly or material functions; their presence alone does not quantify each one's mechanical contribution.
- The brief product context here links to **web.dragline**, **web.scaffold** and **web.silk-proteins** for mechanics and use.

### IMAGE BRIEFS

- Draw the tail, sac and folded duct from the widow micrographs, with a small secretory-cell inset and two duct-cell insets; leave honeycomb-vesicle function labelled unknown.
- Trace this gland to the ALS and one outgoing lifeline; the web context should occupy only a small corner of the anatomical plate.

## 2. body.silk-glands.minor-ampullate — A Smaller Ampulla, A Shared Product

**Suggested kicker:** Small does not mean expendable.

**Suggested summary:** Minor ampullate silk contributes to widow prey wrapping. Direct tests of fibres drawn from its outlets connect this smaller organ to a distinct material, while sequencing reveals more than one MiSp gene.

**Taxa studied:** LATRODECTUS: *L. hesperus*, *L. geometricus* and *L. variolus*; OTHER THERIDIID: *Steatoda grossa* in the expression comparison.

### OBSERVATIONS

- Minor ampullate glands have smaller sacs, shorter tails and folded ducts. **established — LATRODECTUS, L. hesperus** [chaw2018]
- Minor ampullate outlets occur on the posterior median spinnerets. **established — LATRODECTUS, L. variolus** [coddington1989]
- Peptide analysis identified MiSp1-like protein in western-widow prey-wrap material. **supported — LATRODECTUS, L. hesperus** [lamattina2008]
- MiSp1-like transcripts were concentrated in the minor ampullate gland in the original qPCR comparison. **supported — LATRODECTUS, L. hesperus** [lamattina2008]
- The characterized sequence contains alanine stretches interrupted by glycine. **established — LATRODECTUS, L. hesperus, sequence result** [lamattina2008]
- Its C-terminal sequence resembles previously characterized minor ampullate spidroins. **supported — LATRODECTUS, L. hesperus, sequence comparison** [lamattina2008]
- Major and minor ampullate gland protein profiles overlapped more than either overlapped with the tubuliform profile. **supported — LATRODECTUS, L. hesperus, three-gland proteomics** [chaw2015]
- Low ECP3 transcript levels were detected in minor ampullate tissue. **supported — LATRODECTUS, L. hesperus, qPCR** [shanafelt2021]
- At least two MiSp loci were supported in the western-widow genome. **supported — LATRODECTUS, L. hesperus, targeted genomic sequencing** [vienneauhathaway2017]
- Those loci had unequal expression in minor ampullate glands. **supported — LATRODECTUS, L. hesperus and L. geometricus** [vienneauhathaway2017]
- Tested minor fibres differed mechanically among western widows, brown widows and false widows. **supported — LATRODECTUS and OTHER THERIDIID, L. hesperus, L. geometricus and S. grossa** [vienneauhathaway2017]

### TERMS

- **MiSp1-like:** the identification used for the characterized widow minor ampullate protein.
- **qPCR:** a method measuring amplification of a selected nucleic-acid target; reverse transcription permits RNA comparisons.
- **Composite:** a material containing more than one component.
- **C-terminus:** the carboxyl end of a protein chain.

### Claims to avoid

- Do not assign the widow the orb-weaver's temporary construction spiral merely from its gland name.
- These outlet-drawn fibres are not bulk prey-wrap tests; collection, strain definition and repeated fibres per spider matter when comparing their mechanics with other silks.
- A cell-type-resolved widow minor-ampullate atlas and its export-specific non-spidroin inventory were not verified; low ECP3 RNA does not demonstrate ECP3 incorporation into a pure minor fibre.
- The original “restricted” expression result describes that assay's targets and sensitivity, not universal exclusion from every other tissue.
- Male expression does not establish which courtship thread contains MiSp; link to **web.male-silk** and **web.wrapping** for behaviour.

### IMAGE BRIEFS

- Pair source-scaled major and minor organs with the same orientation; label tail, ampulla and duct, and trace only the minor outlet to the PMS.
- Put a sequence strip and a labelled mixed prey-wrap sample beside the gland; do not assign every wrapping filament to MiSp.

## 3. body.silk-glands.aciniform — Many Small Wrapping Factories

**Suggested kicker:** A large protein in a small organ.

**Suggested summary:** Numerous aciniform glands feed the rear spinnerets. Widow molecular work now reaches from identifying their silk in finished products to testing how AcSp1 domains assemble in solution.

**Taxa studied:** LATRODECTUS: *L. hesperus*, including recombinant sequence-derived proteins, and *L. variolus* outlets; OTHER SPIDER: *Argiope argentata* as a separately labelled fibre-structure comparator.

### FIGURES

| Value | Unit | Short label | Qualifier | Sources |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 18,999 | bp | A complete AcSp1 coding sequence | Sequenced *L. hesperus* locus, including termination codon | [ayoub2013] |
| 16 | sequence repeats | Repetition at a different scale | Same locus; repeat units 371–375 amino acids under the 2013 sequence definition | [ayoub2013] |
| 5.0–7.0 | pH interval tested | A different terminal response | Recombinant widow AcSp1 N-terminal domain dimerization remained pH-independent over this interval | [qin2025] |

### OBSERVATIONS

- Aciniform glands lack tails and have fine ducts. **established — LATRODECTUS, L. hesperus** [chaw2018]
- The anatomical study of southern-widow material reported hundreds of aciniform glands. **established — LATRODECTUS, identified as L. mactans in the study** [moon1998]
- Aciniform outlets occur on both posterior spinneret pairs. **established — LATRODECTUS, L. variolus** [coddington1989]
- AcSp1-like protein was identified in both fine egg-case fibres and prey wrapping. **supported — LATRODECTUS, L. hesperus** [vasanthavada2007]
- The complete AcSp1 sequence predicts a protein considerably larger than the earlier electrophoretic estimate. **established — LATRODECTUS, L. hesperus, comparison of methods and specimens** [ayoub2013; vasanthavada2007]
- Low ECP3 transcript levels occur in aciniform tissue. **supported — LATRODECTUS, L. hesperus** [shanafelt2021]
- Recombinant widow AcSp1 repetitive domains form bundles dominated by seven α-helices. **supported — LATRODECTUS, L. hesperus sequence, crystallography** [qin2025]
- Intrinsically disordered linkers promote phase separation in the tested widow AcSp1 constructs. **supported — LATRODECTUS, L. hesperus sequence, in vitro** [qin2025]
- Their N-terminal domain forms concentration-dependent tetramers as well as dimers. **supported — LATRODECTUS, L. hesperus sequence, in vitro** [qin2025]
- Native garden-spider prey-wrap fibres contain substantial α-helical structure alongside β-sheet domains. **supported — OTHER SPIDER, Argiope argentata, solid-state NMR** [addison2018]

### TERMS

- **AcSp1:** aciniform spidroin 1.
- **Intrinsically disordered linker:** a flexible protein segment without one stable folded structure under the tested conditions.
- **Dimer / tetramer:** an assembly of two / four molecular subunits.
- **α-helix / β-sheet:** different forms of local protein backbone organization.

### Claims to avoid

- “Twice as tough as dragline” comes from other spiders and cannot serve as a widow aciniform measurement.
- Neither recombinant-fibre tensile values nor the *Argiope* structural fractions are a native widow wrapping-silk specification.
- The later structural decomposition into folded domains and linkers does not contradict the earlier, longer sequence-repeat definition; label what a “repeat” includes.
- No verified cell-type atlas or complete exported non-spidroin recipe is supplied for widow aciniform glands; ECP3 RNA is a candidate lead.
- Keep attack choreography in **web.wrapping**, and egg-sac architecture in **web.egg-sac-silk**.

### IMAGE BRIEFS

- Show a cluster of small glands feeding PMS and PLS outlets, with a single enlarged gland; use the original scale reference rather than enlarging every gland to the size of the ampulla.
- Make a widow AcSp1 domain plate showing folded helical bundles joined by flexible linkers, then experimental droplet and oligomer schematics; label it recombinant molecular evidence rather than an in-gland movie.

## 4. body.silk-glands.tubuliform — A Factory That Swells for Eggs

**Suggested kicker:** Reproduction changes the organ.

**Suggested summary:** Tubuliform glands make the main egg-case fibres, using a spidroin together with several other proteins. Their changing dimensions and transcription connect gland activity with the reproductive cycle.

**Taxa studied:** LATRODECTUS: female *L. hesperus*; outlet comparison with *L. variolus* and the material identified as *L. mactans* in the 1998 study.

### FIGURES

| Value | Unit | Short label | Qualifier | Sources |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | gland pairs | Several egg-case outlets | Female southern-widow material; two ducts to PMS and four to PLS in total | [moon1998] |
| 125 ± 2 → 340 ± 8 | µm gland diameter | Virgin to gravid | *L. hesperus*, means ± SE; multiple glands from three individuals per reproductive stage, not gland length | [casem2010] |
| 157 ± 7 | µm gland diameter | Smaller after spinning | Same study, post-egg-case glands; mean ± SE, not a refill-time measurement | [casem2010] |

### OBSERVATIONS

- Tubuliform glands are elongated with curled ducts. **established — LATRODECTUS, L. hesperus** [chaw2018]
- Egg-case outlets occur on both posterior spinneret pairs. **established — LATRODECTUS, L. variolus** [coddington1989]
- TuSp1 is a repeat-rich spidroin identified from western-widow egg-case silk. **established — LATRODECTUS, L. hesperus** [hu2005]
- ECP-1 and ECP-2 are distinct non-spidroin components associated with its large fibres. **established — LATRODECTUS, L. hesperus** [hu2006]
- The ECP proteins have cysteine-rich regions consistent with proposed intermolecular linking roles. **supported — LATRODECTUS, L. hesperus, biochemical interpretation** [hu2006]
- ECP3 was identified across gland, dope and egg-sac samples. **supported — LATRODECTUS, L. hesperus, proteomics** [shanafelt2021]
- The same comparison recovered CRP4 and fasciclin among proteins common to those three sample classes. **supported — LATRODECTUS, L. hesperus** [shanafelt2021]
- TuSp1 and ECP-2 transcripts began increasing earlier in vitellogenesis than ECP-1 transcripts. **supported — LATRODECTUS, L. hesperus** [casem2010]
- The glands appeared flattened after egg-case production. **supported — LATRODECTUS, L. hesperus** [casem2010]

### TERMS

- **Tubuliform / cylindrical gland:** alternative names for the egg-case silk gland class.
- **Vitellogenesis:** yolk accumulation during oocyte development.
- **Gravid:** carrying developed eggs.
- **ECP:** egg-case protein; ECP1, ECP2 and ECP3 are not TuSp1 synonyms.

### Claims to avoid

- “Exactly three proteins” is outdated; a classic three-component model is not a complete modern inventory.
- Do not make the gland a single bag filled with a preformed solid thread, or equate diameter with volume or silk mass.
- No verified native widow tubuliform stress–strain dataset is proposed here; recombinant TuSp1 fibres have their own processing history.
- Gland-specific secretion is better supported than a named, cell-resolved widow tubuliform zonation map, which was not verified.
- The virgin category in the developmental paper should not be relabelled “newly hatched,” “before the final molt,” or “unmated for exactly X days.”
- Link to **web.egg-sac-silk**, **web.egg-sac-silk.defence** and **life.egg-sac** for product architecture, brown-widow spikes and maternal behaviour.

### IMAGE BRIEFS

- Draw source-scaled virgin, gravid and post-spinning glands on one plate with diameter brackets across the body, not along its length; place a separately scaled oocyte row beneath them.
- Show gland tissue, expressed dope and sampled egg-case fibres as three distinct experimental sample classes, with TuSp1 and ECPs labelled as molecules rather than coloured anatomical zones.

- Pyriform glands lack tails and have fine ducts. **established — LATRODECTUS, L. hesperus** [chaw2018]

### TERMS

- **Pyriform / piriform:** alternative spellings meaning pear-shaped; not a guarantee that every isolated organ resembles a perfect pear.

- **Enrichment:** greater abundance in a specified comparison, not exclusivity.

- PySp1 alone is not a demonstrated complete adhesive recipe; the original sequence was incomplete and the luminal mixture differed from it.
- No chemically validated full widow pyriform non-spidroin inventory or cell-type-resolved secretion map was verified.
- No isolated widow pyriform-fibre strength value is proposed; disc detachment force depends on geometry, substrate and loading direction.
- Do not infer two gland classes or two different chemistries merely from strong scaffold anchors and releasing gumfoot anchors.

### IMAGE BRIEFS

- Show a sampled attachment junction in cross-section with fine pyriform material around a large structural line; label unsolved cement chemistry instead of colouring it as pure PySp1.

## 6. body.silk-glands.aggregate — Two Glue Factories Per Side

**Suggested kicker:** Related glands, different shapes and expression.

**Suggested summary:** The two aggregate pairs are a distinctive part of the female widow apparatus. Their morphology, gland expression and exported protein mixture offer a factory-level explanation for the adhesive discussed in web.gumfoot.glue.

**Taxa studied:** LATRODECTUS: *L. hesperus* and *L. geometricus*; OTHER THERIDIID: *Steatoda grossa* and *Parasteatoda tepidariorum* in comparative expression/proteomic work.

### FIGURES

| Value | Unit | Short label | Qualifier | Sources |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2 | gland pairs | Anterior and posterior aggregate | Female *L. hesperus* | [chaw2018] |
| 48 | enriched protein groups | Candidate glue components | *L. hesperus*, Glue+Fiber versus dry Fiber; not 48 purified adhesive ingredients | [ayoub2021] |

### OBSERVATIONS

- Anterior aggregate glands have lobed bodies and sheathed ducts. **established — LATRODECTUS, L. hesperus** [chaw2018]
- Posterior aggregate ducts are wider and unsheathed. **established — LATRODECTUS, L. hesperus** [chaw2018]
- Both aggregate pairs feed posterior lateral spinnerets. **established — LATRODECTUS, southern-widow anatomical material** [moon1998]
- Aggregate expression profiles were distinct from fibre-producing glands in the comparative transcriptome analysis. **supported — LATRODECTUS and OTHER THERIDIID, sampled species** [clarke2017]
- AgSp1 fragments were among the abundant proteins in western-widow glue-bearing gumfoot samples. **supported — LATRODECTUS, L. hesperus** [ayoub2021]
- AgSF2 was another abundant component of those samples. **supported — LATRODECTUS, L. hesperus** [ayoub2021]
- AgSF1 and AgSF2 were characterized as aggregate-gland-associated products with architectures different from conventional spidroins. **supported — LATRODECTUS, L. hesperus** [vasanthavada2012]
- Antibody labelling localized these factors at junctions in webs and wrapping silk. **supported — LATRODECTUS, L. hesperus** [vasanthavada2012]
- Widow gumfoot adhesive contains glycoproteins and hygroscopic organic salts. **established — LATRODECTUS, L. hesperus** [jain2015]
- Its tested droplets behaved as a viscoelastic liquid. **supported — LATRODECTUS, L. hesperus, specified laboratory conditions** [sahni2011]

### TERMS

- **Anterior / posterior aggregate:** the two differentiated gland pairs, also called typical / atypical in older literature.
- **AgSp:** aggregate spidroin.
- **AgSF:** aggregate silk factor; a different naming system from AgSp.
- **Viscoelastic:** showing both dissipative flow and elastic recovery, with behaviour dependent on time scale.

### Claims to avoid

- AgSp2, AgSF2 and the older name ASG2 are not interchangeable strings; follow the identity used by each paper.
- Different aggregate shapes or expression do not alone prove which pair supplies every droplet or junction in each behaviour.
- Mixed glue-plus-fibre proteomics is not an assay of purified gland secretion or a complete map of adhesive function.
- Adhesive has rheology and adhesion, not a meaningful dry-fibre “breaking strength” interchangeable with MPa dragline cards.
- No verified cell-type map or ion/water transport budget for each widow aggregate pair is provided.
- Keep humidity response, adhesion tests and gumfoot performance in **web.gumfoot.glue**; this hall adds the paired organs and synthesis evidence.

### IMAGE BRIEFS

- Make a paired anterior/posterior gland plate, tracing their ducts to the two enlarged PLS outlets; use the original dissection as the anatomical reference.
- Show RNA sampled from a gland, antibody-labelled junctions, and Glue+Fiber sampling in separate boxes so visitors do not read them as the same experiment.

Six observations are retained: anatomy, mixed sampling and a protein identification do not justify eight separate functional claims.

### TERMS

- **Flagelliform:** the gland/spidroin class associated with extensible capture silk in familiar orb-weaving comparators.
- **Triad:** the spatial association of a flagelliform outlet with two aggregate outlets.
- **Homology:** similarity interpreted as inheritance from a shared ancestor, distinct from present-day function.
- **Pooled sample:** tissues analysed together, preventing attribution of every result to one constituent.

### Claims to avoid

- Do not draw a widow capture spiral or label its gumfoot core “flagelliform” from an orb-weaver chart; the relevant widow axial-fibre evidence points to major ampullate silk. [blackledge2005b]
- Whole-sac detection does not locate that variant in an individual fibre or prove extrusion from the anatomically identified flagelliform gland.
- Ac + F expression cannot cleanly assign spidroins or non-spidroins to the small flagelliform component.
- No verified isolated widow flagelliform fibre, tensile curve, gland-specific secreted-protein inventory or cell atlas is supplied.
- The retained gland’s current function remains unresolved. **debated — LATRODECTUS, L. hesperus, functional interpretation** [chaw2018; clarke2017]
- Link to **web.evolution** and **web.gumfoot** for capture-system history and operation.

## 8. body.silk-glands.spinning — From Dope to Fibre

**Suggested kicker:** The duct changes the conditions.

**Suggested summary:** Spinning combines molecular assembly with changing flow and chemistry. Widow experiments test several parts of that process directly; measurements from other spiders supply clearly labelled comparisons, not a complete widow production-line map.

**Taxa studied:** LATRODECTUS: *L. hesperus* excised glands, extracted dope, native fibres and recombinant domains; OTHER SPIDER: *Nephila edulis* (original name) and *Euprosthenops australis* for duct measurements.

### FIGURES

| Value | Unit | Short label | Qualifier | Sources |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 10–100 | µm fibre length | Acid-induced assembly | *L. hesperus* extracted major ampullate fluid, in vitro; roughly 2 µm diameter, not natural spinning speed or length | [xu2015] |
| About 200–400 | nm assembly diameter | Dope nanostructures | *L. hesperus*, cryo-TEM after 4 h in 4 M urea; dominant observed population, not direct imaging inside an intact gland | [parent2018] |
| 7.6 → 5.7 | pH | A measured duct gradient | OTHER SPIDER, *E. australis*, proximal tail to second duct limb; not a measured widow gradient | [andersson2014] |

### OBSERVATIONS

- Acidified native widow dope followed nucleation-and-elongation assembly kinetics in vitro. **supported — LATRODECTUS, L. hesperus** [xu2015]
- Changing pH changed assembly rates and the resulting β-sheet fraction. **supported — LATRODECTUS, L. hesperus, in vitro** [xu2015]
- The widow MaSp1 N-terminal domain responds to acidification through altered association and conformation. **supported — LATRODECTUS, L. hesperus sequence, recombinant-domain experiments** [bauer2016]
- Specific acidic residues influenced the domain's dimer stabilization and conformational switching. **supported — LATRODECTUS, L. hesperus sequence, mutagenesis** [bauer2016]
- Diffusion NMR supported entangled spidroin assemblies in excised widow glands. **supported — LATRODECTUS, L. hesperus** [parent2018]
- Cryo-TEM of briefly urea-treated dope revealed hierarchical, approximately spherical assemblies. **supported — LATRODECTUS, L. hesperus, prepared samples** [parent2018]
- Shearing prepared samples changed their internal substructures toward fibrillar organization. **supported — LATRODECTUS, L. hesperus, micropipette-shear treatment** [parent2018]
- Potassium phosphate induced liquid–liquid phase separation of extracted widow dope under experimental conditions. **supported — LATRODECTUS, L. hesperus, urea-containing laboratory preparation** [johnson2025]
- The phase-separated preparation retained disorder without immediately forming β-sheets in the repetitive core. **supported — LATRODECTUS, L. hesperus, NMR** [johnson2025]
- Solid-state NMR detected contacts between arginine and tyrosine in native widow dragline fibres. **supported — LATRODECTUS, L. hesperus** [johnson2025]
- Sodium and chlorine decreased along the golden-silk-spider duct while potassium, phosphorus and sulfur increased. **supported — OTHER SPIDER, N. edulis, elemental microanalysis** [knight2001]
- Polarized microscopy revealed liquid-crystalline textures along the golden-silk-spider major ampullate pathway. **supported — OTHER SPIDER, N. edulis** [knight1999]
- Droplet movement in that pathway supported elongational flow contributing to molecular alignment. **supported — OTHER SPIDER, N. edulis** [knight1999]
- Carbonic-anhydrase inhibition disrupted the measured acid gradient in the nursery-web-spider preparation. **supported — OTHER SPIDER, E. australis** [andersson2014]

### TERMS

- **Nucleation:** initial formation of an assembly that can then grow.
- **Shear / elongational flow:** velocity differences across a fluid / stretching of fluid elements along their path.
- **Liquid crystal:** a fluid with molecular orientational order; not a rigid crystal.
- **Liquid–liquid phase separation (LLPS):** separation into coexisting protein-rich and protein-poor liquid phases.
- **Carbonic anhydrase:** an enzyme catalysing interconversion of carbon dioxide and water with bicarbonate and protons.

### Claims to avoid

- Neither acid alone nor drying in air is an adequate account of natural spinning.
- Widow ultrastructure suggests duct water removal without measuring its rate. [casem2002]
- Elemental phosphorus or sulfur measurements do not by themselves identify every phosphate or sulfate compound, ion pump or transporter.
- No living-widow map of pH, ions, shear and water flux across every duct region was verified.
- Distinguish direct observations from an integrated assembly model; micellar, liquid-crystalline and phase-separation descriptions concern different structural scales and experimental conditions.
- The 2025 phase-separation experiment used extracted, solubilized material and phosphate dialysis; do not paint its buffer concentrations onto an intact widow gland.
- Protein domains and gland classes can differ in their assembly response, as the AcSp1 hall shows; do not apply one MaSp switch to every silk.
- This hall extends **body.spinnerets** and **web.silk-proteins** with internal processing evidence.

### IMAGE BRIEFS

- Build a widow major-gland pathway with solid labels for observed anatomy and separate experimental insets; show water movement as a qualified hypothesis, and put the comparator pH curve in its own species-labelled panel.
- Compare an unordered solution, protein-rich droplets, orientational order and a solid fibre as conceptual states, with no claim that every intermediate has been filmed inside a widow; include the specimen-preparation conditions beside the nanoparticle inset.

## 9. body.silk-glands.genes — A Library Larger Than Seven Recipes

**Suggested kicker:** One name can cover several loci.

**Suggested summary:** Widow research progressed from complete silk genes to tissue transcriptomes and a chromosome assembly. These datasets answer different questions: what DNA is present, what RNA a tissue expresses, and which proteins reach a product.

**Taxa studied:** LATRODECTUS: principally *L. hesperus*, with *L. geometricus* and the separate *L. elegans* genome; OTHER THERIDIID: *S. grossa* in gland-expression comparisons.

### FIGURES

| Value | Unit | Short label | Qualifier | Sources |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 9,390 / 11,340 | bp coding sequence | Two enormous exons | Sequenced *L. hesperus* MaSp1 / MaSp2 loci, not universal lengths for all copies | [ayoub2007] |
| 14 | assembled chromosomes | A western-widow reference | 2024 chromosome-level assembly, 1.46 Gb; assembly length is not a silk-gene count | [miles2024] |

| 26 | annotated spidroins | A separate species inventory | 2022 *L. elegans* assembly and annotation; not the *L. hesperus* count | [wang2022] |

### OBSERVATIONS

- The original complete western-widow MaSp1 and MaSp2 coding sequences each lacked introns. **established — LATRODECTUS, L. hesperus, sequenced loci** [ayoub2007]
- Both encode repetitive interiors flanked by less repetitive ends. **established — LATRODECTUS, L. hesperus** [ayoub2007]
- Multiple presumed functional MaSp1 loci were found in both western and brown widows. **supported — LATRODECTUS, L. hesperus and L. geometricus** [ayoub2008]
- A MaSp1 pseudogene was also recovered from the western widow. **established — LATRODECTUS, L. hesperus, sequenced copy** [ayoub2008]
- Recombination was supported among widow major-ampullate spidroin loci. **supported — LATRODECTUS, L. hesperus, sequence analysis** [ayoub2008]
- The 2014 transcriptome compared combined silk glands with venom glands and cephalothorax. **established — LATRODECTUS, L. hesperus, sampling design** [clarke2014]
- Many of its silk-gland-enriched transcripts lacked an informative functional annotation. **established — LATRODECTUS, L. hesperus, original analysis** [clarke2014]
- The later comparative study separated anterior and posterior aggregate samples but combined aciniform and flagelliform samples. **established — LATRODECTUS and OTHER THERIDIID, three-species study** [clarke2017]
- MaSp1 and MaSp2 transcripts also occurred in western-widow tubuliform and anterior aggregate glands. **supported — LATRODECTUS, L. hesperus** [clarke2017]
- Changes in tissue expression did not require a coincident inferred gene duplication in the analysed gene families. **supported — LATRODECTUS and OTHER THERIDIID, comparative evolutionary analysis** [clarke2017]
- The western-widow chromosome assembly combined long-read, Hi-C and transcriptomic information. **established — LATRODECTUS, L. hesperus, 2024 assembly method** [miles2024]

### TERMS

- **Genome / transcriptome / proteome:** DNA inventory / sampled RNA inventory / sampled protein inventory.
- **Locus / allele / paralog:** genomic position / variant at a locus / related gene produced by duplication.
- **Pseudogene:** a gene-derived copy carrying changes inconsistent with an ordinary functional product.
- **Concerted evolution:** processes making repeat units or related copies more alike than their independent histories would predict.
- **Hi-C:** chromosome-contact information used, among other applications, to help arrange assembled DNA sequences.

### Claims to avoid

- “Seven silk genes,” “exactly two dragline genes,” and “647 genes for making silk fibres” conflate categories.
- A gland-enriched transcript may support secretion, metabolism or cell maintenance; it is not automatically an exported structural protein.
- Non-detection in a small tissue comparison does not prove expression nowhere else, at no other life stage.
- Closely repeated long sequences make assembly, locus counting and distinguishing alleles from paralogs difficult; do not treat every transcript fragment as an independent gene.
- Do not transfer the 26-spidroin *L. elegans* annotation to *L. hesperus*, or call the 2014 transcriptome a complete genome.
- Ancient genome-wide duplication and later silk-gene duplications are different events; the 2024 genome is not proof that a single duplication created seven glands.
- **web.silk-proteins** owns sequence motifs and material design; **web.human-uses** owns the engineering applications.

### IMAGE BRIEFS

- Show DNA, gland RNA and exported protein as three differently labelled evidence tracks, with a sampled gland at the centre; use an example of overlapping expression rather than seven exclusive colour-coded genes.
- Draw the original MaSp coding regions to a common scale with expanded repetitive interiors and distinct terminal regions; place a labelled fragmented-read assembly inset beside them to explain the measurement challenge.

## 10. body.silk-glands.life-and-supply — Growing, Spending, Replenishing

**Suggested kicker:** Production changes with the animal.

**Suggested summary:** Development, sex and feeding alter silk investment at different levels. Widow data show shifts in gene expression and reproductive gland state; the often-repeated stories about rapid refill, efficient recycling and glands regrowing at molt need separate evidence.

**Taxa studied:** LATRODECTUS: *L. hesperus*, *L. geometricus*, *L. hasselti* and *L. mirabilis*; OTHER THERIDIID: *S. grossa*; OTHER SPIDER: *Araneus cavaticus* for molt and recycling experiments.

### FIGURES

| Value | Unit | Short label | Qualifier | Sources |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| >64 | % amino-acid residues | A biased protein demand | Glycine + alanine in each of the original sequenced *L. hesperus* MaSp proteins; not dietary requirements or ATP expenditure | [ayoub2007] |
| 48 | h after egg-case production | Transcripts persist | *L. hesperus*, sampled reproductive time point; not the time to refill a gland | [casem2010] |
| 5 | days of treatment | A short feeding experiment | Paper-identified *L. hesperus*, protein-containing versus sucrose-only solutions | [blamires2014] |

### OBSERVATIONS

- First-instar *L. mirabilis* produced silk webs in the developmental observations. **established — LATRODECTUS, L. mirabilis, author's instar terminology** [barrantes2010]
- Mature males expressed substantial major ampullate, minor ampullate, aciniform and pyriform spidroin RNA. **supported — LATRODECTUS and OTHER THERIDIID, L. hesperus, L. geometricus and S. grossa** [correagarhwal2017]
- Functional tubuliform, aggregate and flagelliform outlets were absent from males in that study's anatomical framework. **supported — LATRODECTUS and OTHER THERIDIID, sampled theridiids** [correagarhwal2017]
- Minor ampullate spidroin expression predominated in mature male western and brown widows. **supported — LATRODECTUS, L. hesperus and L. geometricus** [correagarhwal2017]
- Low expression of unexpected tubuliform- or aggregate-associated genes was nevertheless detectable in some male libraries. **supported — LATRODECTUS and OTHER THERIDIID, study-specific transcript detections** [correagarhwal2017]
- Egg-case protein transcripts remained detectable after female western widows spun egg cases. **supported — LATRODECTUS, L. hesperus** [casem2010]
- Those post-spinning transcript levels do not establish the rate of protein accumulation. **established — LATRODECTUS, L. hesperus, measurement limitation explicitly recognized by the study** [casem2010]
- In southern-widow ampullate epithelium, mechanically stimulated secretion altered secretory-granule abundance and cell appearance. **supported — LATRODECTUS, identified as L. mactans, before/after pulling microscopy** [moon1998]
- Protein deprivation increased capture-thread stickiness in the western-widow-labelled feeding experiment. **supported — LATRODECTUS, paper-identified L. hesperus** [blamires2014]
- A redback experiment detected no feeding-treatment effect on tested major ampullate mechanical properties. **supported — LATRODECTUS, L. hasselti, specified protein-feeding manipulation** [blamires2018]
- Before molt, smaller ampullate glands supplied fibres while the larger glands were nonfunctional in barn spiders. **established — OTHER SPIDER, Araneus cavaticus juveniles, repeated dissections** [townley1993]
- Barn-spider experiments recovered ingested radiolabel in subsequently spun silk. **supported — OTHER SPIDER, A. cavaticus, silk/web feeding experiment** [townley1988]
- Reincorporation into silk was lower than total solubilization of the ingested material in that study. **supported — OTHER SPIDER, A. cavaticus** [townley1988]

### TERMS

- **Instar / stadium:** a developmental stage / the interval occupied by that stage between molts.
- **Proecdysis:** preparation for shedding the old cuticle.
- **Tartipore:** a post-molt cuticular feature associated with the passage of a duct retained to an old spigot during proecdysis.
- **Replenishment:** replacement of exported gland contents; distinguish transcription, protein synthesis and restored usable output.
- **Recycling:** reuse of ingested material; an experiment must specify whether it measures digestion, uptake or reincorporation into new silk.

### Claims to avoid

- Spiderling web production proves functioning silk secretion, not a complete adult-female seven-class inventory; no stage-by-stage widow gland census was verified.
- Female-only functional egg-case machinery does not mean males lack the corresponding DNA or can never transcribe it.
- Adult male outlet differences do not establish the exact moment or histological course of each gland's regression; no widow longitudinal gland-loss series was verified.
- The barn-spider molt mechanism cannot be called demonstrated widow gland regeneration; routine duct remodelling also differs from repairing an experimentally damaged organ.
- No widow gland-class-specific refill interval, maximum sustainable daily output, ATP budget, amino-acid flux balance or injury-regeneration experiment was verified.
- Glycine/alanine-rich silk still requires biosynthesis and cellular work; “nonessential amino acids” does not mean free production.
- Diet effects on glue, dragline, silk quantity and web architecture are separate outcomes; protein deprivation is not total starvation.
- The 2014 paper reports collection in Gainesville, Florida despite identifying its widow as *L. hesperus*; preserve that published identification but flag the locality inconsistency, rather than silently treating it as a locality-verified western-widow population.
- Do not import an orb-weaver recycling percentage or a daily orb-rebuilding cycle into widow biology; no quantified widow silk-ingestion/reincorporation study was verified here.
- Cross-reference **web.hunger** for silk investment, **web.construction** for web persistence, **web.male-silk** for adult behaviour, **web.growing-up** for spiderling web form and **life.molting** for ecdysis behaviour.

### IMAGE BRIEFS

- Show spiderling, adult male and reproductive female as separate, species-verified anatomical study windows, with unmeasured gland complements left unfilled; add a female tubuliform cycle without inventing a refill clock.
- Make an evidence plate contrasting widow feeding and gland observations with labelled barn-spider molt/recycling experiments; any orb belongs only in that explicitly named comparator panel, never around a widow.
